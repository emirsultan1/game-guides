The best gun in ROB RUN isn’t always the most expensive one. Every gun you carry as a criminal is a gun you can lose, so the right choice depends on what you’re doing and what you can afford to lose.

This guide goes by **stage and role**, not just raw damage.

## How guns work in ROB RUN

- Before you can buy a gun, you have to **unlock it in Shipments** (press **H**). Then you buy it from the Black Market. See [how Shipments work](/rob-run/shipments-explained).
- Most guns come in **Mk1, Mk2 and Mk3** versions. Higher Mk usually means a bigger magazine and better stats.
- Some of the best weapons need **accumulated jail time** or a **higher-level settlement** before you can unlock them.

## Stage 1: Your first store runs

**DP-9 pistol.** It’s the cheap starter pistol. It loses to any automatic gun in a real fight, but holding a clerk at gunpoint doesn’t need anything better, and it costs almost nothing to lose.

Other cheap options: the **CB45** pistol and the **Harris 500** shotgun, which is good inside small buildings.

> **Tip:** don’t bring an expensive gun to rob a café. If you die, it’s gone.

## Stage 2: Your first real gun

**KL-545 (best at Mk3).** The best value rifle early on. It’s cheap, it’s versatile, and it handles most fights you’ll get into on small and medium jobs. Almost every tier list puts it as the standout early pick.

**Also good at this stage:**

- **PDW90** or **JP-7**: SMGs if you prefer close range. The PDW90 has a big 50-round magazine even at Mk1.
- **MC-10**: a cheap automatic, useful early but quickly outclassed.

## Stage 3: The main upgrade

**RC 5.56 Mk3.** The best all-round rifle for most players: good at mid range, easy to control, and much cheaper than the top-end guns. If you only save up for one serious weapon, make it this one.

## The best guns by role

| Role                     | Best pick        | Why                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| All-round rifle          | **RC 5.56 Mk3**  | Best balance of power, control and price             |
| Close range (SMG)        | **AP Scalar Mk3** | Highest sustained close-range damage                 |
| Close range (shotgun)    | **TAG-12K Mk3**  | Biggest burst damage if the shot lands               |
| Marksman                 | **SR Reaper Mk3** | Fast, accurate follow-up shots at range              |
| Holding a position       | **LM 249 Mk2**   | Huge magazine; slow to reload and very expensive     |
| Budget                   | **KL-545 Mk3**   | Best value early on                                  |

The **AP Scalar** and **SR Reaper** are rated top tier on almost every list, with the **RC 5.56**, **TAG-12K** and **LM 249** close behind.

See [the best armor](/rob-run/best-armor) for the armor side.

## Tips

- **Match the gun to the job.** Big guns on small jobs only increase what you can lose.
- **Lower your mouse sensitivity** until tracking a moving player feels comfortable. Aim matters more than the gun. See [best settings](/rob-run/best-settings).
- **Weapons reset at the wipe.** Don’t save an expensive gun for “later” on day 6.
- **Cops get free gear.** Playing a shift is a free way to practise with better weapons.
