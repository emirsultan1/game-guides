Making money in ROB RUN is easy. **Keeping it** is the hard part, because other players and the police are always looking to take your bag. As a solo player you can’t win every fight, so the trick is to earn in ways that don’t need one.

Here’s how to go from broke to steady income on your own, in the order that makes sense.

## The money methods at a glance

| Method                | When            | Risk     | What you need               |
| --------------------- | --------------- | -------- | --------------------------- |
| Dumpster looting      | First hour      | Very low | Nothing                     |
| Small store robberies | Early game      | Medium   | Lockpick, Mini Drill        |
| Action Camera         | Early to mid    | Medium   | Cash to buy it, criminal actions |
| Cargo                 | Mid game        | Medium–high | A car with cargo space   |
| Grow Room             | Once you have a plot | Low (passive) | A rented plot and base |
| Police shifts         | Any time        | Very low | An open police slot          |

## 1. Dumpsters: money with no robbery

Searching **dumpsters** around Looty Bay is the lowest-risk way to start. You don’t need a mask, tools or a plan, so you can do it while you learn the map.

It won’t make you rich, but it gets you the cash for your first tools without risking anything.

## 2. Small store robberies: your main early income

Once you have a **lockpick** and a **Mini Drill**, small stores are the backbone of solo money.

**Good solo targets:** convenience stores, cafés, pharmacies, gas stations and jewelry stores in quieter parts of the city.

**Skip as a solo player:** the central bank, the art gallery and other big targets. They’re built for crews.

**How to run a store solo:**

1. **Scout as a civilian** first. Park in the back, not out front.
2. **Cut the alarm** before you go in.
3. **Shoot the cameras.**
4. **Keep the staff at gunpoint** so nobody raises the alarm.
5. **Grab small, valuable items first**: watches, jewelry, laptops, the safe.
6. **Leave the way you came in** and get out of the city.

### Sell in the same visit

Your **Notoriety** rises the longer you stay masked, and the Fence pays more at higher Notoriety. If you rob a store and then sell straight away, you get that bonus. Chain two small stores before selling and the bonus is bigger, but so is the risk.

Always **take the cash off the counter immediately** after selling. Cash left there can be stolen or confiscated.

## 3. Action Cameras: turn crime into profit

The **Action Camera** is one of the best early investments. You buy it, then **fill it by doing criminal actions** at the Notoriety level it asks for. A full camera sells for much more than you paid.

The basic idea:

1. Buy an Action Camera with your first spare cash.
2. Keep it on you while you rob stores.
3. Once it’s full, sell it.
4. Buy the next one, and later move up to the **Code Tablet** and **USB Hacking Tool**, which work the same way.

Each one pays for itself the first time you fill it.

## 4. Cargo: bigger money, more attention

During **cargo events**, containers are marked on the map. Pick them up and either:

- **Sell the cargo directly.** Faster and safer, which is usually better solo.
- **Take it back to your base** and break it down on your **workbench** before selling. This can pay more, but you need a safe base and the right equipment.

Cargo draws other players, so check who’s around before you go for it.

## 5. Grow Room: income while you’re away

Once you’ve rented a **plot**, a **Grow Room** is the best passive income for solo players. It keeps growing while you’re out looting. You still have to harvest it and sell it yourself, but you don’t have to be there while it grows.

The launch patch made base businesses much more profitable, so it’s worth setting one up as early as you can in each wipe.

## 6. Police shifts: the safe fallback

Lost your gear? Join the police for a shift. The department gives you equipment and a car, so you risk nothing of your own, and you earn from arrests and confiscations.

It pays less than a good criminal run, but it’s a reliable way to rebuild. See [Cop vs. criminal: which earns more](/rob-run/cop-vs-criminal-earnings).

## How to keep your money

Earning is half the job. Losing it is what keeps solo players poor.

- **Don’t carry more cash than you can afford to lose.** Sell, collect, and get it somewhere safe.
- **Launder it.** Money in the bank can’t be stolen until the next wipe. See [how to launder money](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money).
- **Avoid the desert with a full bag.** Dying there costs you all your loot.
- **Don’t fight crews.** As a solo player, a fair fight against three people isn’t fair. Leave.
- **Check the wipe timer** before buying anything expensive. Everything resets at the weekly Federal Raid.

## A simple solo day plan

1. **Fresh server:** dumpsters and one small store to buy your tools.
2. **Next runs:** small store, sell, repeat. Buy an Action Camera.
3. **Open a bank account** and start laundering.
4. **Rent a plot** and set up a Grow Room.
5. **From then on:** robberies and cameras for big money, the Grow Room for steady money.
