ROB RUN’s world has two halves:

- **Looty Bay**, the city, where you rob places and the police patrol. See [the Looty Bay map](/rob-run/looty-bay-map).
- **The desert**, a big **global map** of player bases, settlements and districts.

This guide covers the desert.

<!-- CHECK: add global map screenshot here, e.g. ![The ROB RUN global map](/images/rob-run/global-map.jpg) -->

## The desert is lawless

There are **no police** in the desert, and the rules are harsher than in the city:

- **Dying in the desert costs you all the loot you’re carrying**, whatever your role.
- Other players can attack you on the road.
- It’s where everyone’s bases are, so it’s where raids happen.

**Don’t drive around the desert with a full bag.** Sell and launder first.

## How the desert is organized

1. The desert is split into **districts**.
2. Each district is split into **settlements**, laid out as a hex grid.
3. Each settlement has **plots** for player bases.

[SCREENSHOT: the desert map with its districts]

**Highlands**, in the **middle** of the map, is the central district. The clan that holds it rules the server as **Shadow Governor**. See [districts and the Shadow Governor](/rob-run/districts-and-shadow-governor).

**Looty Bay** sits on the **east coast**, with **Lenny’s Motel** and the **prison** outside the city.

## District levels

Every district has a **level: 1, 2 or 3**. Its settlements and plots share that level.

| Level | What it means                                              |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | The starting districts. Rent with a level 1 Land Plot Title |
| 2     | Needs a level 2 title; unlocks better gear and cars        |
| 3     | Needs a level 3 title; unlocks the best gear and cars      |

Higher-level districts also give their controlling clan **faster laundering**.

See [how to rent a plot](/rob-run/how-to-rent-a-plot).

## District hubs

Each district has a **hub** where you can sell loot, as an alternative to the Fence vans in the city.

## How to choose where to settle

When you pick a settlement for your base, think about:

- **Level:** a higher level unlocks better items in Shipments, but needs a better title.
- **Distance to Looty Bay:** a long drive through the desert with loot is a long time at risk.
- **Who controls the district:** a friendly clan’s district is much safer than a hostile one.
- **How busy it is:** quiet settlements get raided less.
- **Your clan:** settle where your clan is, so you can defend each other.

## Desert tips

- **Sell and launder before you drive home.** The desert is where you lose everything.
- **Don’t stop for strangers** on the road.
- **Set a raid alarm** on your base, since there’s no offline protection. See [protecting your base](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).
- **Everything resets** at the weekly Federal Raid, so the map’s power balance changes every week.
