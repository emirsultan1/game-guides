Quick answers to the questions new players ask most. Each one links to a full guide if you want more.

## The game

### What is ROB RUN?

A multiplayer crime sandbox by **Kefir**. You rob stores and banks in the city of **Looty Bay**, or play as a cop chasing real-player robbers, then build a base and businesses in the desert and climb the **Power Pyramid**, from broke Hobo to Shadow Governor.

### When did ROB RUN come out?

On **4 November 2026**, in **Early Access** on Steam.

### When is the full release?

There’s no date yet. The Steam page says Kefir expects Early Access to last roughly **6 to 12 months**.

### Is ROB RUN free?

**Yes.** It’s free to play on Steam. The in-game shop sells **cosmetics only**.

<!-- CHECK: still cosmetics only? -->

### Is ROB RUN pay to win?

No. Paid items are cosmetic, so everything that affects gameplay is earned in the game.

## Platforms

### Is ROB RUN on PS5 or Xbox?

**No.** It’s a **Windows PC** game on Steam only. Kefir hasn’t announced a console version.

### Can I play on Mac, Linux or Steam Deck?

**No.** It’s Windows only, and the anti-cheat blocks Linux and Steam Deck.

### Does ROB RUN support controllers?

**Not officially.** It’s built for keyboard and mouse. Some players map a controller through **Steam Input**, but that’s a workaround, not official support.

### What are the system requirements?

You need **64-bit Windows 10 or later**, a broadband connection and about **20 GB** of free space. Your CPU needs to support **AVX2**, or the game crashes.

See [the best settings for FPS](/rob-run/best-settings) to get it running smoothly.

## Playing with others

### Can I play ROB RUN solo?

**Yes.** You can rob, sell, rent a plot, run a base and play cop alone. Kefir also gives solo players some bonuses to help them keep up with groups. There are no solo-only servers, though: you share the city with everyone.

See [how to make money fast solo](/rob-run/make-money-fast-solo).

### What’s the max party size?

**3 players**, including the leader. Kefir tested bigger parties and decided against them.

### What’s the difference between a party and a clan?

A **party** is your squad of up to 3 for a job. A **clan** is a much bigger group that fights for territory and protects each other’s bases.

See [how to join or create a clan](/rob-run/how-to-join-or-create-a-clan).

## Servers and wipes

### What servers are there?

Official servers only, in two regions: **EU** and **East US**. There are no private or community servers.

### When do new servers open?

At least one **fresh server opens every day** in each region.

### How often does ROB RUN wipe?

Every server wipes **7 days** after it opens, in the **Federal Raid**. Your plot, base, weapons, vehicles and all money reset. Cosmetics, your name, settings and jail time carry over.

See [the Federal Raid](/rob-run/federal-raid-wipe).

### Can I play with friends on another server?

You need to be on the **same server**. Clans are tied to one server too.

## Gameplay basics

### How do I become a criminal?

**Put on your mask.** You’re a civilian until you do, and you stay a criminal until you leave the city.

See [roles explained](/rob-run/civilian-criminal-or-cop).

### How do I become a cop?

Go to the **Police Station** in Looty Bay as a civilian and start duty when a slot is open.

See [how to be a cop](/rob-run/how-to-be-a-cop).

### Where do I sell loot?

To a **Fence**: a van marked with a **dollar sign** on the Looty Bay map. Cargo goes to the **Smuggler** instead.

### How do I keep my money safe?

Open a **bank account** and **launder** your cash. Banked money can’t be stolen by other players.

See [how to launder money](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money).

### What happens when I die?

It depends on your role. In the city, civilians lose nothing, criminals lose their bag, and cops lose only department gear. **In the desert, you lose all the loot you’re carrying.**

### Can my base be raided while I’m offline?

**Yes.** There’s no offline protection, so use fuses and an alarm, and launder your money.

See [how to protect your base](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).
