ROB RUN doesn’t let you build forever. Every server is wiped once a week in an event called the **Federal Raid**, and everyone starts again from nothing. Knowing when it hits, and planning around it, is the difference between a great week and money thrown away.

## When does the wipe happen?

**Every server wipes 7 days after it opens.** Each server has its own clock, so check how old your server is.

New servers open **every day** in each region, so there’s always a fresh one to start on.

## What resets and what you keep

| Wiped at the Federal Raid           | Kept after the wipe         |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Your plot and base                  | Cosmetics                   |
| Weapons and items                   | Your player name            |
| Vehicles                            | Tutorial progress           |
| All cash, **including banked money** | Settings                   |
| Settlements and districts you own   | Accumulated jail time       |

## Jail time and Authority

The wipe doesn’t just delete what you had. It **converts the value of your assets into jail time**, which adds to your **Authority**. That’s the part of your progress that carries on from week to week.

## How to plan your week

### Days 1–2: get set up fast

The start of a server is the best time to play.

- **Cops have weaker gear** on fresh servers, which makes early robberies easier.
- Plots and settlements are up for grabs.
- Do your [first-hour steps](/rob-run/first-hour-in-looty-bay): tools, first money, bank account, a plot.

### Days 3–5: build and earn

- Set up your base and businesses, like a Grow Room.
- Run your best money routes.
- Invest in [base defense](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids), since you’ll be using the base for a few more days.

### Days 6–7: spend, don’t save

- **Check the wipe timer** before any big purchase. A new van on day 6 is money thrown away.
- **Spend your money** on things you’ll use before the wipe.
- Go for the bigger, riskier heists. You have less to lose.
- Wrap up big jobs well **before** the 7-day mark.

## Should you move to a fresh server?

Joining a server late in its week means less time before everything resets. Starting on a **fresh server** gives you the full 7 days and weaker police.

If you only play a few days a week, starting each session on a newer server is often better than catching up on an old one.

## Common mistakes

- **Saving money for next week.** You can’t. All cash resets, even in the bank.
- **Big purchases on day 6 or 7.** You won’t get the value back.
- **Over-investing in base defense** late in the week.
- **Starting on an old server** without checking how long it has left.
