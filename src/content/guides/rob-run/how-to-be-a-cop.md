Playing cop is the low-risk way to earn in ROB RUN. The department gives you free gear and a car, you can’t lose your own equipment on shift, and you get paid for catching criminals. Every criminal you chase is a real player, which makes it a lot more fun than chasing bots.

## How to join the police

1. Enter **Looty Bay** as a **civilian** (no mask).
2. Drive to the **Police Station**.
3. Talk to the officer inside and choose **Start Duty**.
4. If you have a **badge**, pick its better kit. Otherwise you get the standard one.

No unlock is needed for your first shift. In a party, everyone has to be at the station to enlist together.

## Why you can’t join: slots are full

There are only a few police slots per city, and they fill up fast. If you can’t join:

- **Wait** and check back.
- **Answer a backup call.** When the police are short on players, a backup request pops up. Accepting it puts you on duty with **better gear** and a **temporary pay bonus**.
- **Try a fresher server.**

## What you get on duty

- **Standard gear** and a **patrol car**. You can’t use your personal items on shift.
- A **siren** in the patrol car.
- The ability to set up **roadblocks** to close off escape routes.

If you die on duty, you only lose the **department’s gear**. Your own things stay safe.

## How cops get paid

You don’t get a fixed hourly wage. You get paid for lawful actions, mostly catching criminals:

- **Arrests** pay the most: a share of the value of the loot you confiscate. A rich robber is worth much more than a broke one.
- **Kills** pay much less than arrests.
- **Bribes**: a criminal with money in the bank can offer you one. Accept and you get paid, and they keep their bag.

At the end of your shift, part of your pay comes as **confiscated goods** rather than just cash.

## Arrest, don’t kill

This is the most important cop tip. **An arrest pays several times more than a kill.**

You can only arrest a criminal who has **surrendered** (hands up) or been **knocked out**. So:

1. **Turn on your siren.** It gives a temporary bonus on lawful actions and calls nearby officers as backup.
2. **Order them to surrender.** This gives a temporary bonus on the arrest.
3. **Arrest** them while the bonus is active.

Killing a criminal who has already surrendered pays nothing, so don’t.

## Badges: better gear

**Badges** unlock better police kits. They’re used up when you use them, one kit per shift.

- The ranks go **Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain**.
- You can **buy badges**; which ones are on offer depends on the district you live in.
- You can also **find** them, and trade lower badges up for higher ones.

Save your badges for **busy, high-value shifts**, not quiet patrols.

## Tips for your first shift

- **Stay with other cops.** Alone, you’re an easy target for a crew.
- **Go where the alarms are.** Big targets like the bank pull criminals with full bags.
- **Use roadblocks on the exits.** Criminals have to leave the city to keep their loot.
- **Don’t rush in alone** against a full crew. Wait for backup.
- **Fresh servers:** cops start with weaker gear, often just pistols, and better gear unlocks as the server gets older.

## Is it worth it?

Cops earn less than good criminals, by design. But you risk nothing of your own, which makes a shift the perfect way to **rebuild after losing your gear** or to **learn how fights work** before risking your own equipment. See [cop vs. criminal: which earns more](/rob-run/cop-vs-criminal-earnings).
