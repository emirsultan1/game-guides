New players often open the Black Market, see half the items locked, and don’t know why. That’s the **Shipments** tree. Almost every weapon, tool, vehicle and base item has to be unlocked there before you can buy it.

## The two-step system

Getting a new item takes two payments:

1. **Unlock** it in Shipments, **once**, with cash. This only gives you the right to buy it.
2. **Buy** it from the Black Market **every time** you need one.

On the Black Market, each item shows an **UNLOCK** cost and a separate **PRICE**. Locked items say **OPEN SHIPMENTS**.

The unlock can cost much more than the item, or much less. Check both before deciding.

## How to open Shipments

Press **H**, or open it from the side menu. It works in the desert, at Lenny’s Motel and in Looty Bay.

[SCREENSHOT: the Shipments menu]

You buy unlocked items from **Lenny’s laptop** at the motel, or from the **laptop at your base** once you have one.

## What can block an unlock

| Requirement                  | What it means                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cash**                     | The research cost of the item                                 |
| **Earlier items**            | Some unlocks need the item before them in the same branch     |
| **Settlement level**         | Your base has to be in a level 2 or 3 settlement              |
| **Jail time**                | Some top weapons need enough accumulated jail time            |

[SCREENSHOT: an item in Shipments that's locked, with the reason shown]

The **settlement level** requirement is the big one. Moving your base to a higher-level settlement unlocks a whole new set of items, which is why [renting a better plot](/rob-run/how-to-rent-a-plot) matters for your gear too.

## What to unlock first

You can’t unlock everything in one week. The full tree is far too expensive. So pick a direction and unlock what supports it.

**For robbing stores (most players):**

1. **Land Plot Title**: so you can rent a plot and get a base
2. **Light Body Armor**: cheap early protection
3. A **bigger bag**: more loot per run
4. **KL-545**: your first real rifle
5. **Medium Body Armor** and **Steel Plates**

**For a base and production:** the Land Plot Title, the **Laundering Desk** items, **fuses** and the **Grow Room**. Production is the most expensive starting route, so expect it to take longer.

**For police play:** you don’t need much. The department gives you gear for free.

## Does Shipments reset at the wipe?

**Very likely yes.** The official list of what carries over after the weekly **Federal Raid** doesn’t include your unlocks, and players report the tree resetting with everything else.

What does carry over is your **jail time**, which is why some unlocks get easier week by week.

## Tips

- **Don’t unlock things you won’t use this week.** Every unlock is money not spent on gear or a plot.
- **Plan around your settlement level.** No point saving for an item your settlement can’t unlock yet.
- **Unlock early in the week.** You get more days of use from each one before the wipe.
