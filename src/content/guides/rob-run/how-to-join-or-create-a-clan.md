A **party** in ROB RUN is your small squad of up to **three players** for a job. A **clan** is much bigger: a whole group that works together to protect bases, fight for businesses and take over districts. If you want to climb past Settlement Owner in the Power Pyramid, you need one.

## Party vs. clan

|                 | Party                       | Clan                                        |
| --------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Size            | Up to 3 players             | Much larger                                 |
| What it’s for   | Running a job together      | Territory, base defense, long-term goals    |
| Lasts           | One session                 | Until you leave, or the wipe                |
| Server          | Any                         | Tied to the server it was created on        |

A clan doesn’t let you put more than three people in a party. It’s a separate, bigger group.

## Before you start: open a bank account

You need a **bank account** to create or join a clan. Go to the **Looty Bay Bank** as a civilian and open one. See [how to launder money](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money).

## How to join a clan

1. Press **Esc** and open the **Clan** tab in the side menu.
2. Press **JOIN**. The **Clan Search** list opens with the clans on your server.
3. Search by name, or pick one from the list.
4. Press **JOIN**. Open clans let you straight in; others need approval.

You can also be **invited** by a clan member. The official ROB RUN Discord has a channel for clan recruitment too.

**Clans belong to one server.** You and your friends need to be on the same server to be in the same clan.

## How to create a clan

1. **Launder money into your bank account.** Creating a clan uses **laundered money in your bank**, not cash you’re carrying. Laundering takes time, so start early.
2. Open the **Clan** tab and check the requirement shown next to **CREATE**.
3. Press **CREATE**, choose a **name**, an **emblem** and whether the clan is **open** or needs approval to join.
4. **Invite players** by their ID or through chat.

## Clan ranks

Clans have **ranks** with rights you can set, such as:

- Recruiting and kicking members
- Managing the clan **treasury**
- Calling and joining **conflicts** over businesses and territory
- Writing the clan’s daily announcement
- Changing other members’ ranks

Give the important rights only to people you trust. Someone who can manage the treasury can empty it.

## How to pick a good clan

Before you join, ask:

- **How active are they?** A clan that plays at different hours from you won’t help you much.
- **What do they focus on?** Robberies, base building or territory wars.
- **Do they have a base or settlement** you can join?
- **What do they expect from members?** Some want you at every fight.

An active small clan beats a big inactive one.

## What clans are for

- **Base defense:** set your raid alarm to warn your clan, so someone can respond while you’re offline. See [protecting your base](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).
- **Businesses:** clans fight over businesses in the city for steady income.
- **Districts:** a clan that takes a whole district taxes every settlement owner in it. See [districts and the Shadow Governor](/rob-run/districts-and-shadow-governor).

## Remember the wipe

Clan **territory and money** reset at the weekly **Federal Raid** like everything else, so every week the fight for districts starts again.
