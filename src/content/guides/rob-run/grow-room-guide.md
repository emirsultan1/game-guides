The **Grow Room** is ROB RUN’s farming business, and it’s the business most players want first. Once it’s set up, it keeps producing while you’re out robbing, and one good plant keeps paying for the rest of the week.

## What you need

- A **rented plot** with a base. See [how to rent a plot](/rob-run/how-to-rent-a-plot).
- The **Grow Room**, unlocked and bought
- At least one **Grow Table**, unlocked and bought
- **Growing Bushes**, the plants, which you find in the world

## How the Grow Room works

- The **Grow Room** is a building on your base with slots for **Grow Tables**.
- Each **Grow Table** holds several **Growing Bushes**.
- A mature bush gives a few **Harvest Bags**, then **starts growing again** by itself. No replanting.
- You **sell the Harvest Bags** for cash.

<!-- CHECK: sources say a Grow Room holds 6 tables, each table holds 6 bushes, and a bush gives 2–4 Harvest Bags per harvest -->

## Step 1: Unlock and buy the Grow Room

1. Press **H** to open **Shipments** and unlock the **Grow Room** in the production branch.
2. Buy it on the **Black Market** at your base’s laptop.
3. Place it on your plot.

[SCREENSHOT: the Grow Room in Shipments or the build menu]

Then do the same for a **Grow Table**, and place it in the room.

<!-- CHECK: sources say the Grow Room is $150,000 to unlock and $100,000 to buy, and a Grow Table $30,000 to unlock and $40,000 to buy -->

## Step 2: Find Growing Bushes

Growing Bushes are **found in Looty Bay**, inside certain buildings. They count as **cargo**, so you carry them in your hands or in your trunk, not your bag.

[SCREENSHOT: a Growing Bush in the world]

Once you grab one, **go straight home**. Don’t keep looting with a bush in hand. If you die or get caught, you lose it.

<!-- CHECK: where you find bushes. One source suggests a route of the gear store, gun shop and Rusty's Burgers Café. Add your own route. -->

## Step 3: Grow and harvest

1. Place the bush on a free spot on a **Grow Table**. A **clock icon** shows it’s growing.
2. Wait until the clock turns into a **tick**.
3. **Harvest** it. You get **Harvest Bags**, and the bush starts growing again.

[SCREENSHOT: the Grow Room with plants growing]

Better bushes give more valuable bags.

<!-- CHECK: how long a bush takes to mature -->

## Step 4: Sell the bags

Sell **Harvest Bags** to a **Fence** (a van with a dollar sign on the Looty Bay map), or at your district’s **Sales Center**, which you can use once you rent a plot. Then **launder the cash** straight away.

<!-- CHECK: bag values by rarity (sources say about $4,000 Uncommon, $6,000 Epic, $8,000 Legendary) -->

## Grow or sell the bush?

You can also sell a Growing Bush **straight to the Smuggler** as cargo, without growing it. That’s quick cash, but you lose every future harvest.

- **Early in the week:** grow it. It pays back many times over.
- **On the last day or two before the wipe:** sell it to the Smuggler. There isn’t time to get the value back.

## Is it worth it?

**Yes, if you set it up early in the week.** The room and tables cost a lot up front, but a full table of bushes pays that back within a handful of harvests and then keeps earning until the wipe.

The launch patch **massively increased** the profit from base businesses, which made the Grow Room one of the best steady incomes in the game.

## Protect it

Your harvest sits in your base, and bases can be raided. Before you invest:

- Put **fuses** in your Security System and set a raid **alarm**.
- **Sell and launder** often instead of stockpiling bags.

See [how to protect your base](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).
