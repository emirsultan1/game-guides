**Yes, you can be raided while you’re offline.** ROB RUN has no offline raid protection. When players asked the developers about it before launch, their answer was that your main defense is investing in your base’s fortification.

So you can’t stop raids completely. What you can do is make your base **slow, expensive and not worth it** to break into.

## How raids work

Every building on your base has a **Security System**: a panel raiders have to hack.

1. The Security System has a **defense level**: the building’s own protection plus the points from every **Electronic Fuse** you’ve installed.
2. Raiders put **Decoders** into the panel. Each Decoder knocks off some defense.
3. When the defense hits **zero**, they get **temporary access** to the building and can take what’s inside.
4. If the owner gets there in time, they can **repair** the panel and restore the defense, wiping out the raiders’ progress.

Decoders aren’t something you just buy in a shop, so raiders pick their targets carefully.

## What raiders can and can’t take

| In your base                   | Can raiders take it?                    |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Cash stored in the base        | **Yes**                                 |
| Loot and items in storage      | **Yes**                                 |
| Money in your bank account     | **No.** Laundered money is safe         |
| Installed fuses                | No. A hack doesn’t destroy them         |

Wiping a whole base off the map is much harder. It needs an extremely rare item that only drops as loot.

## 1. Fill your Security System with fuses

**Electronic Fuses** go into the Security System panel. Every fuse adds defense points, so raiders need more Decoders and more time.

- Fill **every fuse slot** on the buildings that hold your valuables.
- Better fuses add more points. A building full of top-tier fuses can take raiders a huge number of Decoders to break, which turns a quick raid into a planned operation.
- Fuses **aren’t destroyed** by a hack, and repairing the panel brings the protection back.

## 2. Install a raid alarm

An **Alarm System** warns you when someone starts hacking. There are three levels:

1. Warns **only you**
2. Warns **you and your clan**
3. Warns **every player at the location**

Get the highest one you can afford. A warning while the hack is still running gives you or your clan the time to come back and repair the panel.

## 3. Launder, don’t stockpile

The single best defense: **keep your money in the bank, not in the base.**

Laundered money in your bank account can’t be taken by raiders. Cash sitting in your base can. Run your [Laundering Desk](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money) often, and transfer the money to your account as soon as it’s done.

## 4. Don’t look rich

Raiders have to spend real resources on Decoders, so they pick bases that look worth it.

- **Don’t park expensive cars** out in the open.
- **Don’t brag** in chat or voice about what you’ve got stored.
- A base with nothing worth taking isn’t worth raiding.

## 5. Split your storage

Don’t keep everything in one building.

- Put your **most valuable items** in the building with the **most fuses**.
- Keep **cheap, replaceable things** in the outer buildings.
- If one building falls, you don’t lose everything.

## 6. Use your clan

A clan makes defense much easier:

- Set your alarm to warn **your clan**, so someone can respond even when you’re offline.
- Settle in a **settlement or district your clan controls**.
- If a district turns hostile, **move**. Your plot resets at the wipe anyway.

## Defense checklist

- [ ] Every fuse slot filled on buildings with valuables
- [ ] Alarm System installed, at the highest tier you can afford
- [ ] Laundering Desk running, money transferred to the bank
- [ ] Valuables in the most protected building, not spread around
- [ ] No expensive stuff on display
- [ ] Clan set to receive raid alerts

## Remember the wipe

Everything in your base resets at the weekly **Federal Raid** anyway. Don’t overspend on defense late in a server’s week. See [preparing for the Federal Raid](/rob-run/federal-raid-wipe).
