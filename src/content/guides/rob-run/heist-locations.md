Looty Bay has **28 robbery targets**, from corner stores to the bank vault. They aren’t all worth your time. The right target depends on whether you’re solo or in a crew, what tools you have, and how much attention you can handle.

The developers describe the range as everything from small corner stores and jewelry shops up to bank vaults and police armories. Here’s how they break down.

## Quick overview

| Size            | Examples                                          | Solo?            | Police attention |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Small business  | Convenience stores, cafés, pharmacies, gas station, electronics stores | Yes | Low to medium |
| Houses          | Cottages, houses, mansions                        | Yes              | Low              |
| Medium business | Jewelry stores, gun shops, equipment stores       | Yes, if quick    | Medium           |
| Large business  | Looty Bay Bank, Art Gallery, Grand Hotel, Police Station | Crew only | Very high      |

## Small businesses: your first targets

**Convenience stores, cafés, pharmacies, gas stations and electronics stores.**

These are where every robber starts. They have a safe or two, some cabinets, a clerk and a few cameras. They’re quick, they don’t pull the whole police force, and you can do them alone.

- **You need:** a **lockpick** for the back door, and a **Mini Drill** for bigger safes
- **Best for:** your first runs, and finding **keys** in cabinets that open better rooms elsewhere
- **Tip:** small stores in **Sunny Beach** are a common starting point because they’re close together

## Houses and mansions: quiet money

**Cottages, houses and mansions**, including **Noble’s Mansion** in **Lorraine Hills**.

Remote houses are a favourite for solo players. Police care much more about the big stores downtown, so a quiet house with its alarm disabled can be very low risk. Some houses have no alarm at all.

- **You need:** often nothing, sometimes a **Mini Drill** for the safe
- **Best for:** solo players who want low attention
- **Watch out:** cottages are spread out, so you spend more time driving

## Jewelry stores: the best solo upgrade

There are two: **Jewelry Store East** in **Downtown** and **Jewelry Store West** in **Sunny Beach**.

They don’t need a key. You’re looting cabinets and display cases, and the items are small and valuable, so a full bag is worth a lot. The catch is the **cameras**: you need a fast plan to get in and out.

- **You need:** a fast exit plan; a **glass cutter** helps with display cases
- **Best for:** solo players who’ve done a few small stores
- **Watch out:** police watch the jewelry stores more closely than small shops

## Gun shops and equipment stores

Fast jobs with useful loot: weapons, ammo and gear.

- **You need:** a **room key** for the back rooms, and a **Mini Drill** for the safe
- **Best for:** gearing up as well as making money

## Large targets: crew jobs

### Looty Bay Bank (Downtown)

The classic job. The bank has **large safes**, **deposit boxes** and a **gold vault**.

- **You need:** **Mini Drills** for the safes, a **Drill** for the vault, and the bank’s **ID Card** for the gold
- **The catch:** robbing the vault **alerts the whole city**. Expect nearly every cop on the server to come for you.
- **Crew:** yes. Bring a full party, heavy armor and a getaway plan. See [crew heists](/rob-run/crew-heists).

### Art Gallery (Palmside)

One of the richest targets, with the most loot spots in the city and a **Fabergé Egg** in the vault.

- **You need:** a **keycard** for the vault; a **Glass Cutter** for the egg
- **The catch:** a timed crack and heavy police response
- **Crew:** yes

## Which target should you rob?

- **Your first runs:** a small store or a remote house.
- **Solo with some experience:** jewelry stores, gun shops, houses with a Mini Drill.
- **With a crew of 2–3:** the bank, the gallery, the hotel.
- **When the city is full of cops:** remote houses, away from downtown.

## Where to sell

Sell everything to the **Fence**: a van marked with a **dollar sign** on the Looty Bay map. Sell in the same visit to keep your Notoriety bonus, and take the cash off the counter straight away.
