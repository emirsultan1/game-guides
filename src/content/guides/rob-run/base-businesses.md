Robbing stores is fast money, but it’s risky and it stops the moment you log off. **Base businesses** keep earning in the background. Since the **4 November** patch, which massively increased their profits, they’re one of the best ways to make money in ROB RUN.

All of them need a **rented plot** first. See [how to rent a plot](/rob-run/how-to-rent-a-plot).

## What renting a plot unlocks

Becoming a **Settlement Resident** gives you:

- A **base** where you can build businesses
- New items in **Shipments** that motel players can’t unlock
- The **Sales Center**, where you can sell goods
- **Cheaper contraband**

## The four base businesses

| Business                  | What it does                                       | Start-up cost | Best for                      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Cargo cracking**        | Opens cargo for its contents at a Cargo Workbench  | Low           | Your first business           |
| **Contraband**            | Repacks cheap contraband into valuable cargo       | Low to medium | Steady profit without robbing |
| **Grow Room**            | Grows bushes that produce Harvest Bags, then regrow | Medium        | Passive income all week       |
| **Chemical Lab**          | Turns Chemical Reagents into product                | Highest       | Players with money to invest  |

### Cargo cracking

A **Cargo Workbench** opens the crates, suitcases and safes you steal, so you keep what’s inside instead of selling them sealed. It’s cheap to set up, which makes it the best first business. See [cargo: sell it or open it?](/rob-run/cargo-guide).

### Contraband

Buy cheap **Contraband Cargo** at your district’s Sales Center, repack it at a **Contraband Workbench**, and sell the result to the Smuggler for more. A simple loop that doesn’t need a robbery.

### Grow Room

Your farm. Place **Growing Bushes** on **Grow Tables**, harvest the bags, sell them. Bushes regrow by themselves, so one setup keeps paying all week. See [the Grow Room guide](/rob-run/grow-room-guide).

### Chemical Lab

The most expensive business. It uses **Chemical Reagents** to make product. Save it for when your other income is running and you have money to spare.

## Where to sell what you make

- **Fence vans** in Looty Bay (dollar sign on the map): for normal goods and Harvest Bags
- **The Smuggler** (box icon): for cargo
- **The Sales Center** in your district: better prices, but only for residents of that district, and busy Sales Centers can attract ambushes

## Which business should you start first?

1. **Cargo Workbench:** cheap, and it makes every cargo you find worth more.
2. **Grow Room:** your steady income for the rest of the week.
3. **Contraband:** once you’re comfortable running a loop.
4. **Chemical Lab:** only once the rest is paying for itself.

## Tips

- **Start early in the week.** Businesses need time to pay back what you spent. Everything resets at the Federal Raid.
- **Protect your base first.** Products sitting in your base can be stolen. See [how to protect your base](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).
- **Sell and launder often.** Don’t build up a pile of product or cash.
