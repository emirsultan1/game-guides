The biggest scores in ROB RUN, like the **Looty Bay Bank vault** and the **Art Gallery**, aren’t built for solo players. They take time, they alert the police, and one person can’t open the vault, watch the staff and guard the door at once.

Parties go up to **three players**. Here’s how to use them.

## The three crew roles

These aren’t official classes. They’re a simple way to split the work so nobody is doing two jobs at once. The developers’ help center suggests the same split: breaking in, hauling loot and providing cover.

| Role     | Job                                                   | Carries                         |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Breacher | Opens doors, safes and the vault                      | Lockpicks, Mini Drills, the Drill, keys and cards |
| Hauler   | Grabs the loot and carries it out                     | An empty, large bag             |
| Cover    | Watches the staff, the entrance and the street        | The best weapon and armor       |

### Breacher

Goes in first, disables the electrical panel, picks the locks and starts the drills. Needs every tool for the job. If the breacher forgets the Drill, the heist is over before it starts.

### Hauler

Empties safes and cabinets as fast as they open. Starts with an **empty bag** with as much space as possible. A Sports Bag adds carry space.

### Cover

Keeps the **staff at gunpoint** so nobody calls the police, then moves to watch the entrance and the street for cops and rival crews. Has the best armor, because they’re the first one shot.

## Two players? Combine roles

With only two of you, one player is **breacher and hauler**, and the other is **cover**. That’s enough for most jobs, but bring the third player for the bank.

## Before the heist: the plan

Agree on all of this **before anyone puts on a mask**:

1. **The target**, and what tools it needs.
2. **Who does what.**
3. **Where you park**: behind the building, with the getaway car facing the exit.
4. **The escape route** out of the city, and a backup.
5. **Where you sell** once you’re out.
6. **What happens if it goes wrong**: who surrenders, who runs, where you meet.

Scout the target as **civilians** first. Nobody treats unmasked players as criminals.

## Gear checklist

- [ ] Lockpicks for doors and containers
- [ ] Mini Drills for safes, one per safe you plan to open
- [ ] The **Drill** and **ID Card** if you’re doing the bank vault
- [ ] Heavy armor and extra plates for the cover player
- [ ] An empty, large bag for the hauler
- [ ] A fast getaway car with trunk space
- [ ] Money in the bank, so anyone caught can try a bribe

## The Looty Bay Bank vault

The bank is the classic crew job, and the hardest.

**What it needs:**

- **Mini Drills** for the large safes
- A **Drill** for the vault
- The bank’s **ID Card** for the gold

**What happens:** robbing the vault **alerts the whole city**. Expect nearly every cop on the server to head your way.

**How to run it:**

1. **Scout** the bank unmasked: entrances, staff, where the police come from.
2. **Park** the getaway car close, pointing out.
3. **Mask up** out of sight.
4. **Cover** takes the staff. **Breacher** heads for the vault. **Hauler** follows.
5. Open the safes and vault, load the hauler, and **get out fast**. Don’t get greedy: every extra minute brings more police.
6. **Leave the city** by your planned route and sell as soon as you’re out.

## The Art Gallery

The other big crew target. It has the most loot spots in the city and a **Fabergé Egg** in the vault, which needs a **keycard** and a **Glass Cutter**. The crack is timed, so the cover player needs to hold the building while it runs.

## When it goes wrong

- **Don’t all die in the building.** If the police arrive in force, the hauler leaves first with whatever’s in the bag.
- **Surrender beats dying** if you’re caught: you lose your bag but keep your equipped gear.
- **Bribes** work only if you have money in the bank.
- **Rival crews** may wait outside to rob you. Cover watches the street, not just the door.
