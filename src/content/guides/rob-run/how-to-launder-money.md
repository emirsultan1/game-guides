In ROB RUN, cash in your pocket is never safe. Other players can steal it and the police can confiscate it. **Laundering** is how you move that cash into your bank account, where it’s protected until the next wipe.

The developers’ own help center sums it up: open a bank account at the Looty Bay Bank as a civilian, then run your cash through the Laundering Desk at your base.

## What you need

- A **bank account** at the Looty Bay Bank
- A **Laundering Desk**, which comes with a base on a rented plot
- Or, before you have a base, **Lenny’s laundering service** at the motel

## Step 1: Open a bank account

1. Go to **Looty Bay** as a **civilian**: mask off. If you’re masked, leave the city first to drop your criminal status.
2. Go to the **Looty Bay Bank** and open an account at the counter.

One visit is enough, and you don’t need a plot first.

A bank account also lets you **bribe the police** when you’re caught, and you need one to **found a clan**.

## Step 2: Launder at your Laundering Desk

Once you have a base:

1. Go back to your base and use the **Laundering Desk**.
2. Move the cash you want to clean from your inventory into the desk.
3. Start laundering and wait. It isn’t instant.
4. When it’s done, use **Transfer to my account** to move the clean money into your bank balance.

### Your money is in three different places

This is where new players get confused:

| Where the money is        | Safe?                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Cash in your bag          | **No.** Can be stolen, confiscated or lost when you die |
| Cash inside the desk      | Only as safe as your base. Bases can be raided          |
| Money in your bank account | **Yes**, until the next wipe                           |

Money only counts as safe once it’s in the bank. Don’t leave a pile sitting in the desk.

## No base yet? Use Lenny

Before you have a plot, you can launder through **Lenny** at the motel. The catch: **he takes a cut**. It’s still better than carrying cash around and losing all of it.

Once you have a base, use your own desk and keep the full amount.

## How to launder faster

Add **Banknote Counters** to your base. They speed up your Laundering Desk, and better counters speed it up more.

Your position in the **Power Pyramid** also matters: higher tiers, like District Owners and the Shadow Governor, get better laundering terms. As a Settlement Resident, part of what you launder goes to the settlement owner as tax.

## Getting your money back out

Need cash for gear? Withdraw it at any **ATM**, or from a bank employee.

Only withdraw what you’re about to spend. The moment it’s cash again, it can be stolen again.

## The money loop that keeps you rich

1. **Rob** a target.
2. **Sell** to the Fence in the same visit.
3. **Take the cash off the counter** straight away.
4. **Get out** of the city.
5. **Launder** it at your base.
6. **Transfer** it to your bank account.

Follow this every time and a bad run only costs you what you were carrying, not everything you’ve made.

## What laundering can’t protect you from

**The weekly wipe.** Every 7 days the **Federal Raid** wipes everything, including the money in your bank. Laundering protects you from other players, not from the wipe. Spend your money before the server resets.

## Common mistakes

- **Carrying a big stack of cash around** instead of laundering it.
- **Leaving money in the desk** and forgetting to transfer it.
- **Withdrawing everything at once** before you know what you’ll buy.
- **Saving money for the next wipe.** You can’t. It resets.
