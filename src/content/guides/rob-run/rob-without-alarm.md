Most robberies in ROB RUN go wrong in the first ten seconds. Someone breaks a window, a camera spots them, or the clerk calls it in, and suddenly the police are on the way before the safe is even open.

A quiet robbery gives you time. Here’s how to do it.

## The five things that set off the alarm

1. **The building’s alarm system**, if you don’t disable it first
2. **Security cameras** that see you
3. **Breaking glass** to get in
4. **Staff** calling the police
5. **Taking loot** before the alarm is off, in houses especially

Deal with all five and you can rob most small targets without anyone coming.

## Step 1: Scout without your mask

Drive to the target as a **civilian**. Look for:

- The **electrical panel**: a small **orange box**, usually at the back or side of the building
- The **back door**
- Where the **cameras** are pointing
- Where the **staff** stand
- Your **way out of the city**

Park **behind** the building, away from the street, so your car isn’t on display.

> **Tip:** don’t put your mask on until you’re out of sight and ready to go. A masked player standing around outside is easy to spot.

## Step 2: Disable the electrical panel

Before you touch anything, go to the **electrical panel** and disable it. On many buildings this turns off the **alarm** and the **cameras** at the same time.

[SCREENSHOT: the electrical panel at a store]

Not every building has one, and not every alarm can be turned off this way. If there’s no panel, move on to the cameras.

## Step 3: Shoot the cameras

Any camera still working can raise the alarm the moment it sees you. **Shoot them before they see you**, from outside or the doorway if you can.

[SCREENSHOT: a security camera inside a store]

Jewelry stores have several cameras, so plan which ones you’ll take out first.

## Step 4: Get in with a lockpick, not glass

- **Use a lockpick** on doors and locked containers. Lockpicking **doesn’t trigger the alarm**.
- **Don’t break windows or glass** to get in. Breaking glass can set off the alarm.

[SCREENSHOT: lockpicking a door (the lockpick minigame)]

A lockpick is cheap and you’ll use it on almost every job. Always carry one.

## Step 5: Keep the staff under control

Clerks and residents will call the police if you let them.

- **Hold them at gunpoint** the whole time you’re inside. That stops them raising the alarm.
- **Don’t kill them** unless you have to. Killing staff fills your wanted level faster and brings the police sooner.

In a crew, one person’s only job should be watching the staff.

## Step 6: Loot fast and smart

- Go for **small, valuable items** first: jewelry, watches, cash, the safe.
- **Drill the safe** with a Mini Drill while the staff are covered.
- Don’t fill your bag with low-value items just because they’re there.

## Step 7: Leave the way you came

Walk out the **back door**, get in your car and drive out of the city calmly. **Don’t crash into other cars** on the way: that draws attention.

Your loot only counts once you’re **out of the city alive**. Leaving also drops your criminal status.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Scouted as a civilian
- [ ] Parked behind the building
- [ ] Mask on out of sight
- [ ] Electrical panel disabled
- [ ] Cameras shot
- [ ] In with a lockpick, no broken glass
- [ ] Staff at gunpoint
- [ ] Safe drilled, valuables first
- [ ] Out the back and out of the city

## If the alarm goes off anyway

It happens. When it does:

1. **Stop looting.** Grab what’s in your hand and go.
2. **Leave by the route you planned**, not the main road.
3. If the police catch you, **surrender** rather than fight: you keep the gear you’re wearing. With money in the bank, you can try a **bribe**.
