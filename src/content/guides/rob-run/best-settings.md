In ROB RUN, fights are often decided before anyone shoots: who spotted who first, and whose game stuttered at the wrong moment. These settings aim for two things: **more FPS** and **seeing other players clearly**.

All of these are in **Esc → Settings**.

## Step 1: Unlock your frame rate

ROB RUN can sit at 60 FPS even on a strong PC until you change two things in **Graphics → General**:

1. Turn **VSync** off.
2. Set **Frame Rate** to **Unlimited**, or to your monitor’s refresh rate (for example 144 or 240).

Press **Confirm** to apply.

## Step 2: The settings that give the most FPS

Not every setting matters. These are the ones worth lowering first:

| Setting                 | Set to    | Why                                                        |
| ----------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shadows                 | Disabled  | The single biggest FPS gain. Lowering it isn’t enough; only *Disabled* helps much |
| Additional Light        | Off       | Big gain, small visual loss                                |
| HBAO                    | Off       | Extra shading you won’t miss in a fight                    |
| Environment Reflections | Off       | Free FPS                                                   |
| Post-processing         | Off       | Cleaner image and more FPS                                 |
| Anti-aliasing           | Off or low | Some FPS back; turn it up if edges bother you             |
| LOD Crossfade           | Off       | Removes the fade between detail levels                     |
| Water                   | Low       | You rarely fight near water                                |
| Grass                   | Minimum   | Also makes players lying in grass easier to see            |

## Step 3: Settings to keep high

Some settings are worth the FPS because they help you see:

- **Object Visibility Distance: High or Maximum.** Players and loot appear further away instead of popping in late.
- **Render Resolution: 1.0** if your PC can handle it. Lower it to **0.8** only if you can’t hold a steady frame rate.
- **Texture Quality:** keep it as high as your graphics card’s memory allows. It costs little FPS.

## Step 4: Fix stutter when moving the mouse

If your FPS drops when you move the mouse quickly, the cause is often a **very high mouse polling rate**. In your mouse’s software:

1. Try **1000 Hz**.
2. Still stuttering? Try **500 Hz**.

## Step 5: Control settings worth changing

In **Controls**:

- **Center-of-screen Marker While in Vehicle: On.** Keeps a marker in the middle of the screen, which makes shooting from a car much easier.
- **Hold to Sprint: On.** Fewer accidental stops while running.
- **Motion Blur: Off** (if your version has it). It hides movement, and movement is what gives players away.
- **Lower your mouse sensitivity** until tracking a moving player feels comfortable. ROB RUN is a shooter before it’s a crime game.
- **Keep the mask and voice keys easy to reach.** You’ll press them under pressure.

The one key every player should know: **H opens the Shipments window.**

## Step 6: Audio

Sound tells you where danger is before you see it.

- Turn on the **Sound Effect Visualizer** if you play without a headset, or as backup. It shows where sounds come from.
- Lower the **music** so footsteps, cars and gunshots stand out.
- Use a headset if you can.

## Quick setup for low-end PCs

If you’re on an older PC or a laptop:

- Use a low **Graphics Quality** preset as your base
- Shadows **Disabled**, Additional Light **Off**, HBAO **Off**
- Level of Detail around **0.8**
- Render Resolution **0.8**
- Water and Grass at **minimum**
- Object Visibility Distance at **Medium**
