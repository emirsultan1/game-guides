You’ve rented your first plot. The mistake most new players make now is spending everything on businesses and nothing on protection, then losing it all to the first raid.

Your base is a **warehouse and a money machine**, not a trophy room. A base that survives a raid is worth more than one that looks impressive.

## How building works

Everything on your base is an **item you place from your inventory**. You buy what you need, then put it down on your plot. Buildings, storage, desks and production are all placed this way.

## The build order

### 1. Storage

Somewhere to keep your loot and gear that isn’t your bag or the motel. Put it in the **building you’ll protect the most**.

[SCREENSHOT: a storage box in a base]

### 2. Security System and fuses

Before anything valuable goes in, set up the **Security System** on your main building and **fill every fuse slot** with **Electronic Fuses**. Raiders have to hack through these, and there’s no offline protection.

Add an **Alarm System** so you, and later your clan, know when someone starts breaking in.

See [how to protect your base from raids](/rob-run/protect-base-from-raids).

### 3. Laundering Desk

Now your money can start moving to the bank, where raiders can’t touch it. Run the desk often and transfer what’s done.

[SCREENSHOT: the Laundering Desk placed in a base]

See [how to launder money](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money).

### 4. Workbench

A **workbench** lets you work on items at your base, like breaking down stolen **cargo** before you sell it. It’s useful, but it isn’t urgent.

[SCREENSHOT: the Workbench in a base]

### 5. Production: Grow Room and more

Once your valuables are safe and your money is flowing to the bank, add **production**. A **Grow Room** is the classic first business: it keeps growing while you’re out robbing. The launch patch made base businesses much more profitable, so it’s worth it once the basics are covered.

### 6. Upgrades

With everything running:

- Better **fuses** on your main building
- **Banknote Counters** to launder faster
- A higher-tier **Alarm System**

## Build order at a glance

| Order | Build                          | Why                                           |
| ----- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Storage                        | Somewhere to keep loot off your body          |
| 2     | Security System + fuses + alarm | Protect it before it’s worth stealing        |
| 3     | Laundering Desk                | Move money to the bank, out of raiders’ reach |
| 4     | Workbench                      | Cargo and items                               |
| 5     | Grow Room / production         | Income while you’re away                      |
| 6     | Upgrades                       | Faster laundering, harder to raid            |

## Tips

- **Don’t build everything in one building.** Keep the most valuable things where the most fuses are.
- **Don’t look rich.** Expensive cars parked outside invite raiders.
- **Check the wipe timer.** Everything resets at the weekly Federal Raid. Big base investments on day 6 are wasted.
- **Settlement owners** get to place their base for free, which is one reason to buy a settlement later. See [settlements explained](/rob-run/settlements-explained).
