The top two tiers of ROB RUN’s **Power Pyramid** can’t be reached alone. **District Owner** and **Shadow Governor** belong to **clans**, and they’re fought over every week.

## The Power Pyramid, from the top

| Tier              | Who                                              | What they get                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Shadow Governor   | The leader of the clan holding **Highlands**     | The best laundering terms and a cut of every other clan’s taxes |
| District Owner    | A clan holding a whole **district**              | A cut of every settlement owner’s tax there, plus faster laundering |
| Settlement Owner  | A player who bought a settlement                 | Tax from their residents                           |
| Settlement Resident | A player renting a plot                        | A base                                             |
| Hobo              | Everyone at the start                            | A bed at Lenny’s Motel                             |

Settlement Owner is the highest you can get on your own. Above that, you need a clan. See [how to join or create a clan](/rob-run/how-to-join-or-create-a-clan).

## How the money flows

The Power Pyramid is a **tax chain that flows upward**:

1. **Residents** pay their **settlement owner** when they launder money or buy from the Black Market.
2. **Settlement owners** pay a cut to the **clan that controls their district**.
3. **District owners** pay into the **Shadow Governor’s** clan.

So the higher your clan climbs, the more of everyone else’s activity ends up in your pocket.

## What a district gives your clan

- A **cut of the taxes** every settlement owner in the district collects
- **Faster laundering** for your clan, better in higher-level districts
- **Control** over a big part of the map, which also makes your members’ bases safer

## How clans fight

Clans compete in **conflicts**: organised battles between clans over **businesses** in the city and **districts** in the desert.

- **Businesses** in Looty Bay give the clan steady income while it holds them. A clan of just **three** players can already start going after businesses.
- **Districts** are bigger fights, with larger teams on each side.
- Battles are won by **capturing objectives**.

Members with the right **rank** can call conflicts and join them.

## How to become Shadow Governor

There’s **only one** Shadow Governor per server:

1. Build a strong, **active** clan.
2. Take and hold districts to build income and laundering power.
3. Win control of the central **Highlands** district.
4. The **leader** of the clan holding Highlands becomes the **Shadow Governor**.

Everyone else on the server is now paying into your clan, which also makes your clan the biggest target on the map.

## It all resets every week

District and Highlands control reset at the weekly **Federal Raid**. Every week, the fight starts over.

That means:

- **Move fast** on a fresh server: districts are up for grabs.
- **Spend** the clan’s income during the week. It won’t carry over.
- A clan that’s organised from day one has a big head start.

See [the Federal Raid](/rob-run/federal-raid-wipe).

## Tips for clans going for territory

- **Activity beats size.** A smaller clan that’s online together wins more fights than a big one that isn’t.
- **Set ranks carefully.** Only trusted members should call conflicts or manage the treasury.
- **Protect your members’ bases.** Set raid alarms to warn the clan.
- **Start with businesses** to build income before going for districts.
