ROB RUN has three roles: **civilian**, **criminal** and **police officer**. You don’t pick a class once and stay in it. You choose every time you go into Looty Bay, and the role you pick decides what you can do and what you lose if you die.

There’s also a second ladder people confuse with roles: the **Power Pyramid**, which starts at **Hobo**. That’s about what you own, not how you play. Both are explained below.

## The three roles at a glance

| Role       | How you become one                          | Best for                                  | What you lose if you die in the city |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Civilian   | Enter Looty Bay without a mask              | Scouting, learning routes, the bank       | Nothing                              |
| Criminal   | Put on your mask                            | Robberies, heists, Notoriety              | Your bag contents                    |
| Police     | Enlist at the police station when a slot is open | Patrols, confiscations, safer income | Only department-issued gear          |

## Civilian: the safe way to learn

Everyone enters Looty Bay as a civilian. You stay one until you put on your mask.

**What it’s good for:**

- **Scouting.** Look at stores, cameras, exits and police routes without anyone treating you as a target.
- **Banking.** You open your bank account as a civilian.
- **Learning the map** before you risk anything.

**The catch:** civilians don’t earn much. It’s a role for preparing, not for making money.

## Criminal: where the money is

Put on your mask and you’re a criminal. You **stay a criminal until you leave the city**, so only mask up when you’re ready to act.

**What it’s good for:**

- Robbing stores, and later bigger heists with a crew
- Building **Notoriety**: the longer your masked spree lasts, the better the loot and the Fence’s payouts, but the more attention you get

**What you risk:**

- Die in the city and you **lose your bag contents**.
- Get caught and you can **surrender**: the police take your bag, but you keep the gear you’re wearing.
- With money in the bank, you can try to **bribe** the cop instead. If they accept, you keep everything.

> **Tip:** never mask up while you’re still scouting. A masked player standing outside a store is an invitation for police and other criminals.

## Police: steady income, lower risk

Real players are the police in ROB RUN, not bots. To join, go to the **Looty Bay Police Station** and enlist when a slot is open.

**What you get:**

- **Standard equipment and a patrol car**, issued by the department
- A **salary**, plus money from **confiscations** and **bribes**
- **Badges** from patrol jobs, which unlock better equipment

**What you risk:** if you die on shift, you only lose the **department’s gear**. Your own equipment stays with you. The trade-off, as the developers put it, is that cops risk less but **earn less** than criminals.

**The catch:** police slots are limited and often full, so you may have to wait or try another server.

## Which role should you start with?

**Start as a civilian.** Spend your first runs learning one area of the city: one small store, its cameras, and the fastest way out.

**Then pick based on how you like to play:**

- Want the biggest payouts and don’t mind losing your bag sometimes? **Criminal.**
- Want to practise fighting and earn without risking your own gear? **Police.**
- Playing with friends? A crew of criminals can take bigger targets, and parties go up to three players.

Most players end up doing both: police shifts when they’re low on gear, criminal runs when they’re ready to take a risk.

## Roles vs. the Power Pyramid (Hobo explained)

“Hobo” isn’t a role. It’s the first tier of the **Power Pyramid**, ROB RUN’s ladder of what you own:

1. **Hobo:** no money, no home, sleeping at Lenny’s Motel. Everyone starts here.
2. **Settlement Resident:** you rent a plot and build a base.
3. **Settlement Owner:** you own a whole settlement and earn from its residents.
4. **District Owner:** your clan controls a district and takes a cut from its settlements.
5. **Shadow Governor:** the leader of the clan holding the central district. There’s only one.

You can be a civilian, criminal or cop at any tier. The roles are how you earn; the pyramid is what you spend it on. Everything resets at the weekly wipe, so everyone climbs again each cycle.

## Quick death-rules reminder

| Role     | Die in Looty Bay      | Die in the desert |
| -------- | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| Civilian | Nothing               | All your loot     |
| Criminal | Bag contents          | All your loot     |
| Police   | Department gear only  | All your loot     |
