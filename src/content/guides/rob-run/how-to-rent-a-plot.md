Renting a plot is the moment you stop being a **Hobo** sleeping at Lenny’s Motel and become a **Settlement Resident** with a base of your own. It unlocks storage, a Laundering Desk and businesses like a Grow Room.

You don’t need to buy a whole settlement. Renting one plot inside someone else’s settlement is enough.

## What you need

- A **Land Plot Title** for the right district level
- A **settlement that already has an owner**
- A **vacant plot** in that settlement

## Step 1: Get a Land Plot Title

A **Land Plot Title** is the document that lets you rent a plot. Since the **4 November update**, renting in a level 1 district costs **documents instead of cash**.

Plots come in **three levels**, matching the **level of the district** they’re in:

| District level | What you need                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Level 1        | A level 1 Land Plot Title                        |
| Level 2        | A level 2 title, a special document from the Black Market |
| Level 3        | A level 3 title, a special document from the Black Market |

Buy the titles from the **Black Market** on Lenny’s laptop.

## Step 2: Find a settlement with an owner

Open the **global map** and look for a settlement that **already has an owner**. You **can’t rent** in a settlement nobody owns: it doesn’t even open to residents until someone claims it.

Make sure the settlement’s **district level** matches your title.

## Step 3: Pick a vacant plot

Travel to the settlement and find an **empty plot**. Before you pick one, think about:

- **Distance:** how far it is from Looty Bay and the roads you use most.
- **Neighbors:** a quiet settlement is safer than a busy one full of crews.
- **Who controls the district:** if a hostile clan runs it, think twice.

## Step 4: Pay at the sign

Every plot has a **sign right next to it**. Use it to pay the rent with your title. Once it’s confirmed, the plot is yours and you can start building.

## Why can’t I rent a plot?

| Problem                              | Fix                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| The settlement has no owner           | Find one that’s been claimed                         |
| Your title is the wrong level         | Get a title that matches the district level          |
| No vacant plots                       | Try another settlement                               |
| You’re at the wrong plot’s sign       | Use the sign right next to the plot you want         |

## Moving to a better plot

Want to move up to a level 2 settlement? Get a **level 2 Land Plot Title** and rent there. Your base moves with you.

## Remember the tax

As a resident, part of the money you launder and the Black Market purchases you make goes to the **settlement owner** as tax. See [settlements explained](/rob-run/settlements-explained).

## Don’t forget the wipe

Your plot resets at the weekly **Federal Raid**, like everything else. Rent early in a server’s week to get the most out of it. See [the Federal Raid](/rob-run/federal-raid-wipe).
