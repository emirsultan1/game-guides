A **settlement** is a player-run area out in the desert, split into plots that players rent for their bases. Settlements are the middle of ROB RUN’s **Power Pyramid**: they’re where most players live, and where owners start earning from other people’s crime.

## How the map is organized

- The desert is split into **districts**.
- Each district contains many **settlements**.
- Each settlement is split into **plots**.

Each district has a **level** (1, 2 or 3), and every settlement and plot in it shares that level. **Highlands**, in the middle of the map, is the central district.

## Resident vs. owner

|                    | Settlement Resident                         | Settlement Owner                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| How                | Rent one plot with a **Land Plot Title**    | Buy the whole settlement with a **Settlement Title** |
| Your base          | On your rented plot                         | A **free** plot for your own base             |
| Tax                | Pay a share to the owner                     | Collect a share from every resident           |
| Pyramid tier       | 2nd                                         | 3rd                                           |

## How settlement tax works

Residents pay the owner a share whenever they:

- **Launder money**, and
- **Buy from the Black Market**

The owner collects it **in person** at the **Debt Collectors’ Van** in the settlement, and carries it out as cash. That cash can be stolen like any other, so launder it.

## How to buy a settlement

1. **Check the global map** for an **unclaimed settlement** first. Buying the title doesn’t reserve one.
2. Buy a **Settlement Title** that matches the **district level**.
3. Go to the unclaimed settlement and **use the title** to claim it.
4. It opens for residents, and you can place your base on a **free plot**.

## Is owning a settlement worth it?

**Worth it when:**

- You have the money early in a server’s week.
- The settlement is in a good spot that residents will want to move into.
- You want income that doesn’t depend on you robbing everything yourself.

**Not worth it when:**

- It’s late in the week. Everything resets at the **Federal Raid**.
- Nobody rents in your settlement. No residents means no tax.

You don’t need a settlement to get started. Most players should rent first and buy later.

## Above settlements: districts

A **clan** that controls a whole **district** becomes the **District Owner**. It takes a cut of the tax every settlement owner in that district collects, and gets faster laundering. The clan holding the central district, Highlands, leads as the **Shadow Governor**.

The tax flows upward: **residents pay settlement owners, and settlement owners pay their district’s clan.**

Districts are a clan fight. Settlement Owner is the highest tier you can reach on your own.
