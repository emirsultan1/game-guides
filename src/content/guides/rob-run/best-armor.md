Armor in ROB RUN is a buffer: it soaks up hits **before** your health does. Like your guns, it’s also something you can lose. The trick is wearing enough to survive the job without wearing more than you can afford to lose.

## How armor works

- Your character has **100 health**.
- **Body armor** adds extra protection on top.
- Armor is split into **segments**. Hits break segments first, then your health.
- **Steel Plates** restore broken segments, even in the middle of a fight.

## Every body armor compared

| Armor                 | Protection | Segments | Best for                                    |
| --------------------- | ---------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Light Body Armor      | 25         | 1        | First store runs, cheap to lose             |
| **Medium Body Armor** | 50         | 2        | **Everyday robberies. The best value**      |
| Assault Body Armor    | 75         | 3        | Jewelry stores and harder solo jobs         |
| Heavy Body Armor      | 100        | 4        | Crew heists and base raids                  |
| Bastion Body Armor    | 100        | 4        | The bank vault: takes **less damage** from every hit |

### Unlocking better armor

Better armor needs a **higher-level settlement** to unlock in **Shipments**:

- **Light:** available anywhere
- **Medium:** level 1 settlement
- **Assault:** level 2
- **Heavy:** level 3

So moving up in settlements isn’t just about your base. It unlocks better gear too.

## Steel Plates: always carry spares

**Steel Plates** fix broken armor segments.

[SCREENSHOT: Steel Plates in the inventory]

- You can use one **while moving**. Jumping won’t cancel it.
- You **can’t** use one on armor that’s still fully intact.
- Better plates restore more: an Uncommon plate restores one segment, a Legendary plate two.
- Put them in a **quick slot** before the job, so you’re not digging through your inventory mid-fight.

Spare plates often matter more than the armor itself. A Medium vest with three plates outlasts a Heavy vest with none.

## Which armor should you buy?

- **First runs:** Light, or nothing. Learn the jobs before you invest.
- **Most of the time:** **Medium** plus spare plates. Good protection, and it doesn’t hurt much to lose.
- **Solo jewelry stores and tougher jobs:** Assault.
- **Crew heists and base raids:** Heavy, or Bastion if you have it.

## Don’t forget the rest of your kit

- **Medical supplies** for health. Plates fix armor, not health.
- A **Sports Bag** for more carry space, so you can bring more loot home.
- **Pouches** for extra small items like plates and meds.

## Tips

- **Surrender keeps your gear.** If you’re caught as a criminal, surrendering means you lose your bag but keep the armor you’re wearing.
- **Cops get free armor.** Practising fights on a police shift costs you nothing.
- **Armor resets at the wipe.** Don’t buy a Bastion vest on day 6.
