You can’t reach Looty Bay on foot, so a car is the first thing you need. The good news: you get one free. Owning a better one takes a plot and some money, and it’s worth knowing the steps before you spend.

## Step 1: Use the free rental

If you don’t have an active car, rent one **free** at a **Parking Terminal**. There’s one outside Lenny’s Motel, and you can also use one at your base.

The free car is slow and has a tiny trunk, but it costs nothing to lose. Use it until you have a plot.

## Step 2: Get a plot with a house or garage

You **can’t buy your own car** without a place to keep it. You need a **plot** with a **house or garage** on it.

- A **small garage** has one parking space.
- A **large garage** has two, plus extra storage.

See [how to rent a plot](/rob-run/how-to-rent-a-plot).

## Step 3: Unlock the car in Shipments

Press **H** to open **Shipments** and find the **vehicles** section. Pay once to unlock the car you want.

- Each car unlocks **after the one before it** in its line.
- **Tier II and Tier III cars** also need your base to be in a **level 2 or level 3 settlement**.

See [how Shipments work](/rob-run/shipments-explained).

## Step 4: Buy the key

Unlocking doesn’t give you the car. Open the **Black Market** on the **laptop at your base**, go to **Vehicles**, and buy the car’s **key**.

## Step 5: Put the key in your Key Locker

Go home and put the key in an empty slot of your **Key Locker**. The car then **appears in your garage**, ready to drive.

Car keys can also turn up as **loot**, which is a cheap way to get one.

## Don’t lose your loot when you switch cars

When you take out a different car, **your current car is put away and anything left in its trunk drops on the ground**. This includes the free rental.

**Always empty the trunk before switching cars.**

## Your car isn’t a safe

The trunk is for carrying loot from the job to the Fence, not for storing it. A car can be stolen, destroyed or searched. Get valuables into your base storage, or better, sell and launder them.

## Which car should be your first?

For most players, the **Longhorn Van**: it’s the cheapest car, with the biggest trunk and plenty of cargo space, so it pays for itself fast. See [the best cars](/rob-run/best-cars) for the full comparison.

## Remember the wipe

Your car resets at the weekly **Federal Raid** with everything else. Buying an expensive car late in a server’s week is money wasted.
