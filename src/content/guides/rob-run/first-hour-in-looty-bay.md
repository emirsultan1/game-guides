## Where you start

Every new player starts as a Hobo, fresh out of prison and broke, sleeping at Lenny’s Motel. Treat the first hour as learning the map, not as making money.

## Your first cash, step by step

1. **Talk to Lenny** and take the free vehicle from the parking terminal.
2. **Scout unmasked.** Drive past small stores and note exits and police routes.
3. **Hit a small store**, not a bank or gallery — those need a crew.
4. **Get out and launder** before spending.

> **Watch out:** police are real players. They can chase you, block roads and arrest you — don’t rob near a station.
