Every new player in ROB RUN starts at the very bottom: a **Hobo**, fresh out of prison, with no money and nowhere to live except a bed at **Lenny’s Motel**. The first hour isn’t about getting rich. It’s about learning the city, getting your first bit of cash, and not losing it.

Follow these steps in order and you’ll end the hour with money in the bank and a plan for your first plot.

## Before you start: the one rule that matters

You are a **civilian** until you put on your mask. As a civilian you can walk and drive around Looty Bay safely, look at targets, and visit the bank. The moment you mask up, you become a **criminal**, and you stay one until you leave the city.

So: **scout with the mask off, rob with the mask on.** Most new players die because they wander around masked before they have a plan.

## Step 1: Finish Lenny’s intro quests

Talk to Lenny at the motel and finish his intro quests before you do anything else. They teach you how roles work, how selling works and how the Shipments window works (press **H** to open it).

[SCREENSHOT: Lenny and the first intro quest on screen]

The motel is also the only place you can store cash before you have a base, and Lenny’s laptop is where you buy tools from the Black Market.

### About Lenny’s $1,000,000 debt

Lenny tells you that you owe him **$1,000,000** for breaking you out. **Don’t rush to pay it.** It doesn’t block anything you need early on, and that money is far more useful spent on tools and a plot.

## Step 2: Take the free car

You can’t reach Looty Bay on foot. Use the yellow **Parking Terminal** outside the motel to get a free rental car when you don’t own one.

[SCREENSHOT: the yellow Parking Terminal outside the motel, with the free rental car]

It’s slow and has a small trunk, but it’s free. Two things to know:

- You can’t modify it.
- When you buy your own car, the rental is taken back and **anything left in its trunk drops on the ground**. Empty it first.

## Step 3: Scout Looty Bay without a mask

Drive into the city as a civilian and look around:

1. Pick **one small target**: a convenience store, café or pharmacy.
2. Note the **back door**, the **cameras** and where the **employees** stand.
3. Plan your **way out**: which road leaves the city fastest, and where the police usually come from.
4. Find the **Fence**: a van marked with a **dollar sign** on the map. That’s where you sell.

> **Watch out:** other players are a risk even when you’re a civilian. This is a shared city, and the police are real people, not bots.

## Step 4: Buy the tools for your first job

Open Shipments (**H**) and unlock **Lockpicks**, then buy a few from the Black Market on Lenny’s laptop. A lockpick opens doors and containers without setting off the alarm.

[SCREENSHOT: the store menu with the Lockpick and starter tools]

That’s all you need for a first store: cabinets, registers and small safes. The **Mini Drill**, which cracks the heavy safes in locked rooms, needs your base in a level 2 settlement, so it comes later. See [every robbery tool](/rob-run/robbery-tools).

Don’t buy expensive weapons yet. A gold weapon in your bag is a gold weapon you can lose.

## Step 5: Rob one small store

Mask on, go in through the back, and work in this order:

1. **Disable the alarm.**
2. **Shoot out the cameras.**
3. **Keep the employees under threat** the whole time.
4. **Empty the cabinets and register**, and open any small safe you have a key for.
5. **Leave the way you came in**, and get out of the city.

Rob **one** store, not the bank or a gallery. Bigger targets need a crew and better gear.

## Step 6: Sell straight away

Drive to the Fence van and sell your loot. Then **take your cash off the counter immediately**. Cash sitting there, or in your bag, can be stolen by other players or taken by the police.

The Fence pays more the higher your **Notoriety**, which rises the longer you stay masked. More Notoriety means better payouts, but also more attention, so don’t push it on your first run.

## Step 7: Open a bank account

Leave the city to drop your criminal status, then come back as a **civilian** and open an account at the **Looty Bay Bank**. One visit is enough.

[SCREENSHOT: the bank screen where you open your account]

A bank account unlocks:

- **Laundering**, which moves your cash into the bank where other players can’t steal it
- **Bribes**, so you can pay a cop to let you go
- **Founding a clan** later on

You don’t need a plot to open an account.

## Step 8: Save for your first plot

Your next goal is to stop being a Hobo. Renting a **plot** in a settlement makes you a **Settlement Resident** and gives you a base with storage, a Laundering Desk and production.

Until then, launder through Lenny (he takes a cut) and keep your cash at the motel.

## What you lose when you die

| Role     | Die in Looty Bay                  | Die in the desert |
| -------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Civilian | Nothing                           | All your loot     |
| Criminal | Your bag contents                 | All your loot     |
| Police   | Only department-issued gear       | All your loot     |

If the police catch you as a criminal, **surrendering** is usually better than dying: they take your bag, but you keep the gear you’re wearing.

## Don’t forget the weekly wipe

Every server is wiped **7 days** after it opens, in the **Federal Raid**. Plots, bases, weapons and all cash, even banked money, are gone. Your cosmetics, name and settings stay.

So the best time to start is the first day or two of a fresh server, and you should check the wipe timer before any big purchase. Buying an expensive van on day 6 is money thrown away.
