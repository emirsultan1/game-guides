A robbery isn’t done when the safe is empty. **Your loot only counts once you’re out of Looty Bay alive.** You stay a criminal until you leave the city, and the cops chasing you are real players who know the roads as well as you do.

Here’s how to get out with your bag.

## Before the robbery: plan the exit

Most escapes are won before the alarm ever goes off.

- **Know both exits.** The city has **two main bridge exits**. Cops can block one, so you need a backup.
- **Pick your route** from the target to each exit, ideally on side streets.
- **Park facing the way out**, behind the building, so you don’t waste seconds turning around.
- **Listen first.** Sirens, speeding cars or gunshots nearby mean the police are already close. Pick another target or wait.

## During the chase

### Break line of sight

Cops can’t chase what they can’t see. Use **side streets, alleys, buildings and parking areas** to break their view and change direction. Don’t stay on one predictable main road.

### Use the second bridge

Police can set up **roadblocks** and close bridges on the main routes out. If your planned exit is blocked, **switch to the other bridge** instead of trying to force through.

> **Watch out:** the exit you used to get into the city might not be open when you try to leave.

### Use your car’s handling

Your car is more useful than fighting several cops on foot. Criminal cars can be more agile in tight streets than police cruisers, so use turns and narrow roads where they’re weaker. If your car is stuck or boxed in, **get out and run**: on foot you can cut through places cars can’t.

### Don’t make it worse

- **Don’t crash into other cars.** It slows you down and draws attention.
- **Don’t kill civilians** on the way out. Civilian kills raise your wanted level, and at a high enough level the whole city can see where you are.

## Once you’re out

Cross the city exit and you’re no longer a criminal. Your loot is safe from the police.

Then **sell it and launder the money** as soon as you can. See [how to launder money](/rob-run/how-to-launder-money).

## When to stop running

Sometimes escaping isn’t worth it. If you’re cornered:

| Option     | You lose                      | Best when                                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Surrender** | Your bag contents          | Your gear is worth more than the loot       |
| **Bribe**  | Bank money for the bribe       | Your bag is valuable and the cop accepts    |
| **Fight**  | Everything in your bag if you die | You’re in a crew and can actually win    |

**Surrendering keeps the gear you’re wearing.** Dying in the city costs you your bag, and dying in the desert costs you all your loot. With a small bag and good gear, surrendering is usually the smart move.

A **bribe** needs money in your bank account. If the cop accepts, you keep everything.
