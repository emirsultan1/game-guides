The right tool turns a locked door into loot. The wrong one, or none, means walking past the best safe in the building. Here’s every robbery tool in ROB RUN, what it opens, and how to get it.

## Quick reference

| Tool                 | Opens                                              | How to get it                    |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Lockpick**         | Doors, containers and car trunks, without the alarm | Unlock in Shipments, then buy   |
| **Mini Drill**       | Heavy safes inside locked rooms, the bank’s safes  | Unlock in Shipments (needs a level 2 settlement), then buy |
| **Drill**            | The Looty Bay Bank vault                           | **Barter only**                  |
| **Glass Cutter**     | The Art Gallery exhibit                            | **Barter only**                  |
| **Master Key**       | Other players’ protected storage                   | Unlock in Shipments, then buy    |
| **Decoders**         | Other players’ base security systems               | Loot only                        |

## Lockpick

Your most-used tool. A Lockpick opens **ordinary doors and containers without setting off the alarm**, and it can also open **other players’ car trunks**, police cars included.

It’s cheap and one of the first things you should unlock. Always carry several.

<!-- CHECK: sources say $5,000 to unlock and $99 each, stacking to 20 -->

## Mini Drill

Cracks the **heavy safes** you find inside **locked rooms**, plus the **large safes** in the Looty Bay Bank. You need one in your inventory for each heavy safe you want to open.

[SCREENSHOT: the Mini Drill being used]

**Important:** the Mini Drill is a **Tier II** unlock. You need your base in a **level 2 settlement** before you can unlock it. On your first runs, stick to cabinets, registers and small safes.

<!-- CHECK: sources say $100,000 to unlock and $18,000 each, after the Lockpick -->

## Drill

The only way into the **Looty Bay Bank vault**. It’s slow and very noisy, so the whole crew has to hold the bank while it runs.

You **can’t buy** the Drill. You **barter** for it (see below).

## Glass Cutter

Cuts any glass cleanly. You need it to take the **exhibit** in the **Art Gallery**.

Like the Drill, it’s **barter only**.

## Master Key

Opens **other players’ protected storage**. It’s expensive, and whether you can unlock it depends on where you live.

## Fill-up items: tools that pay twice

Three items start **empty** and **fill up** as you commit crimes. Once full, you can **sell them for a lot**, or **barter them** for the big heist tools.

| Item                 | Fills up when you…                          | Use it for                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Action Camera**    | Commit crimes at the Notoriety level it asks for | Sell full, or barter for the Drill |
| **Code Tablet**      | Crack safes in Looty Bay                    | Sell full, or barter for the Glass Cutter |
| **USB Hacking Tool** | Hack computers in locked rooms              | Sell full, or barter for the Drill |

Carry them on every run so they fill as you go. See [how to make money fast solo](/rob-run/make-money-fast-solo).

> **Don’t sell your fill-up items by mistake.** A full Code Tablet sold to the Fence is a Glass Cutter you no longer have.

## The suspicious door: bartering for heist tools

Next to **Lenny’s Motel** is a **suspicious door**. It’s a barter trader: instead of cash, it wants specific items.

[SCREENSHOT: the suspicious door / barter screen]

- **Glass Cutter:** trade **filled Code Tablets**.
- **Drill:** trade a **filled Action Camera** and **filled USB Hacking Tools**, plus some cash.

<!-- CHECK: exact recipes. Sources say 3 full Code Tablets for a Glass Cutter, and 1 full Action Camera + 2 full USB Hacking Tools + $50,000 for a Drill. -->

## Keys

Keys open **specific** rooms and safes in named buildings. They’re random loot, not something you buy. See [every key in ROB RUN](/rob-run/keys-guide).

## Which tools to get first

1. **Lockpicks**: right away.
2. An **Action Camera**: fills while you rob, then pays well.
3. **Mini Drills**: once you’re in a level 2 settlement.
4. **Code Tablets** and **USB Hacking Tools**: when you’re working toward a Glass Cutter or Drill for a crew heist.
