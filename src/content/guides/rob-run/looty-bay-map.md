**Looty Bay** is ROB RUN’s city: every robbery, police chase and cop shift happens here. Knowing the map is the difference between a clean getaway and driving straight into a roadblock.

The city sits on **three islands along one main road**:

- **West island:** Sunny Beach and Loraine Hills
- **Middle:** Palmside
- **East island:** Downtown


## The four areas at a glance

| Area          | What’s there                                         | Best for                          | Risk          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Downtown      | Looty Bay Bank, most shops, gas station, cafés       | Beginner store runs, the bank     | Medium to very high |
| Sunny Beach   | Hotels, jewelry, gun and equipment shops, the pier   | Mid-level solo runs               | Medium        |
| Palmside      | Police Station, Art Gallery, hospital                | Crew jobs only                    | Very high     |
| Loraine Hills | Cottages, luxury houses, a large mansion             | Quiet solo runs                   | Low           |

## Downtown

The busy commercial heart of the city, on the east island.

**What’s here:**

- **Looty Bay Bank**, in the north of Downtown. The city’s biggest vault and the Gold Vault job.
- **Jewelry Store East** and the **East gun shop**
- **Electronics store**, **pharmacy** and **convenience stores**
- A **gas station**, a favourite spot for police
- Two **cafés**

**Best for:** your first store runs. There are lots of small targets close together.

**Watch out:** cops gather around the gas station, and the bank pulls the whole police force when it’s hit.

## Sunny Beach

Hotels and shops along the waterfront and pier, on the west island.

**What’s here:**

- The **Grand Hotel**, **Reef Hotel** and **Seasons Hotel**
- The **Strip Club**
- **Jewelry Store West**, the **West gun shop** and an **equipment store**
- A **pharmacy**, **convenience store** and a café on the pier

**Best for:** solo players moving up from small stores. Lots of mid-value targets.

## Palmside

The middle of the city, home to the two highest-risk targets.

**What’s here:**

- The **Police Station**, where you join the police, and where the armory is
- The **Art Gallery**, one of the richest targets in the game
- The **Hospital**

**Best for:** crew heists. This is the last place to be masked up alone.

## Loraine Hills

Quiet luxury houses on the west island.

**What’s here:**

- **Cottages**, including several numbered ones
- **Luxury houses** and a large **mansion**

**Best for:** quiet solo runs. Police focus on the stores downtown, so remote houses are lower risk, but you spend more time driving between them.

## Key places to know

| Place                 | What it’s for                                     | Where                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Fence vans** ($)    | Selling loot                                      | Several around the city, marked with a dollar sign |
| **Smuggler’s vans**   | Selling cargo                                     | A few around the city                  |
| **Looty Bay Bank**    | Opening a bank account, the bank heist            | North Downtown                         |
| **Police Station**    | Joining the police                                | Palmside                               |
| **ATMs**              | Withdrawing banked money                          | All over the city                      |
| **Parking Terminals** | Getting a free rental car                         | All over the city                      |
| **City exits**        | Leaving the city, dropping criminal status        | **Exit East** and **Exit West**        |

## The two exits

Looty Bay has **two exits**: **Exit East** and **Exit West**. You need to leave by one to keep your loot and drop your criminal status.

Police can **block bridges and set up roadblocks**, so always know where both exits are from wherever you’re robbing. See [how to escape the police](/rob-run/escape-the-police).

## Map tips

- **Park facing the exit** when you rob Downtown.
- **Find the nearest Fence van** before you mask up, so you can sell in the same visit.
- **Rob far from the Police Station** when you’re solo.
- **Watch the gas station** downtown: it’s where cops hang out.
