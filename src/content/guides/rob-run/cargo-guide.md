**Cargo** is the big, bulky loot in ROB RUN: crates, suitcases, safes, money bags, statues and plants. It doesn’t fit in your bag; you carry it in your hands or haul it in your trunk. It can be worth much more than normal loot, if you get it home.

## The Fence vs. the Smuggler

Don’t confuse the two:

[SCREENSHOT: the Fence and the Smuggler selling screens]

- The **Fence** buys **normal loot** from your bag: jewelry, electronics, cash.
- The **Smuggler** buys **cargo**. Smuggler vans are marked on the map with a **box icon**, usually in **covered car parks**.

## Your two choices

| Option            | You get                         | Speed                            | Needs                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Sell to the Smuggler** | Cash, straight away      | Instant                          | Just a Smuggler van       |
| **Open it at a Cargo Workbench** | The items inside, often worth more | From seconds up to an hour | A plot and a Cargo Workbench |

Since the **5 November** balance patch, opening cargo at a workbench generally pays **more** than selling it sealed.

**Sell when:** you don’t have a plot yet, you need cash now, or the wipe is close.
**Open when:** you have a base and can wait for the timer.

## The Smuggler pays more with Notoriety

Like the Fence, the Smuggler pays more the higher your **Notoriety** during that city visit. Selling right after a masked run gets you a better price.

## How to get a Cargo Workbench

1. **Rent a plot.** A workbench is base furniture.
2. Press **H** and unlock the **Cargo Workbench** in Shipments.
3. Buy it on the **Black Market**.
4. Place it on your plot.

[SCREENSHOT: the Cargo Workbench]

Then bring cargo home, put it on the bench and wait for it to open.

## The Valuable Cargo event

**Valuable Cargo** is a city event and one of the best single payouts in the game:

[SCREENSHOT: the Valuable Cargo event notice or marker]

1. A notification warns you a few minutes **before it starts**, and the spot appears on the **map**.
2. When it starts, the container unlocks in a **car trunk**.
3. The **first player** to pull it out keeps it, so expect a fight.
4. Get it out of the city and either sell it to the Smuggler or open it at your workbench.

<!-- CHECK: event timing (sources say a 3-minute warning and 10 minutes long) and its value (sources say $150,000 to the Smuggler) -->

## Contraband: buy cheap, sell high

Once you rent a plot, your district’s **Sales Center** sells cheap **Contraband Cargo**. With a **Contraband Workbench** at your base, you can repack it into more valuable cargo and sell that to the Smuggler for a profit.

It’s a steady money loop that doesn’t need a robbery at all.

<!-- CHECK: sources say Contraband Cargo costs $10,000 and repacks into cargo the Smuggler pays $35,000–$40,000 for -->

## How to keep your cargo

Cargo is slow and obvious to carry, so it’s easy to lose.

- **Use the right car.** The **Longhorn Van** has the most cargo space for the price. See [the best cars](/rob-run/best-cars).
- **Go straight to the Smuggler or home.** Don’t keep looting with cargo in your trunk.
- **Cops can seize cargo**, so get out of the city fast.
- **Empty your trunk before switching cars.** Anything left in it drops on the ground.
- **Avoid dying in the desert:** you lose everything you’re carrying.
