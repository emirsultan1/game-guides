In ROB RUN, your car does two jobs: it **carries the loot** and it **gets you out of the city**. No car is best at both, so the right pick depends on how you play.

New to cars? Start with [how to get your own car](/rob-run/how-to-get-a-car).

## What the stats mean

- **Top speed:** how fast you go on a straight road. Great on the highway, less useful in tight streets.
- **Acceleration:** how quickly you get up to speed. Huge for getaways.
- **Handling:** how well you turn. In the city, this matters as much as speed.
- **Trunk rows:** space for loot and bags.
- **Cargo slots:** space for cargo containers, for cargo runs.

## Best first car: Longhorn Van

The **Longhorn Van** is the cheapest car you can buy and has the **biggest trunk** and plenty of **cargo slots**. It’s slow, but it pays for itself quickly on cargo runs and store jobs.

Pick the **Fukurou Tensa** instead only if you want quicker getaways and don’t carry much.

## Best getaway car: Royal J

The **Royal J** is the best balance of **acceleration, handling and space**. It’s quick off the line, turns well in tight streets, and still carries a decent haul. For crew heists it’s the car to aim for.

On a budget, the **Royal E** is a more balanced city car than anything at Tier I.

## Fastest car: Buletti Soltempra

The **Buletti Soltempra** has the **highest top speed** in the game and is the best car for outrunning the police on open roads.

**The catch:**

- It’s **extremely expensive**.
- It has a **tiny trunk** and **no cargo space** at all.
- Its **handling is weak**, so it’s less dominant in tight city streets.

It’s a car for players who already have money and want to escape with small, valuable loot, not for hauling.

## Best cargo car

- **Longhorn Van:** the best value. Add **cargo racks** and it carries even more.
- **Longhorn Titan:** the upgrade. Similar cargo space, much better speed and handling.

## Best car for cops

Cops get a **patrol car** for free on duty. The **Longhorn Wild** line is known as the strongest pursuit option, and the only one that can keep up with a Soltempra.

## Upgrades: what to buy first

Cars take **modules** under the hood, in the trunk and underneath.

- **For getaways:** upgrade **acceleration** and **handling** first. They matter more in the city than top speed.
- **For cargo:** add **cargo racks** to carry more.
- **Speed upgrades** like turbo help most on long highway escapes.

## Which car should you buy?

- **Just starting:** the free rental, then the **Longhorn Van**.
- **Cargo runs:** Longhorn Van, then the **Longhorn Titan**.
- **Robberies and getaways:** **Royal E**, then the **Royal J**.
- **Rich and want speed:** **Buletti Soltempra**.

And remember: every car resets at the weekly **Federal Raid**. Expensive cars are best bought early in a server’s week.
