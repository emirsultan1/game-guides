Lost a pile of cash after one bad police chase? That’s how GTA 6 works: money in your pocket isn’t safe. The fix is simple once you know where to put it.

## What you lose when you die or get busted

| Where the money is | Wasted or busted |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash in your pocket** | **Lost, all of it** |
| **Stolen loot you’re carrying** | Usually lost too |
| **BuckMe account** | **Safe**, minus a small fee |
| **Floorboard stash** | **Safe** |
| **Family Savings** | **Safe** |

Each character can carry **up to $20,000** in cash, and Jason and Lucia have separate wallets. That’s a lot to lose in one crash.

## Before you have a bank: the floorboard stash

Early in Chapter 1 you get cash before you get a bank account. Until then, use the **loose floorboards in Jason’s bedroom** at his house in **Key Lento**:

1. Go to **Jason’s bedroom**.
2. Interact with the **loose floorboards**.
3. Put your cash in.

Money in the stash is safe if you die or get arrested.

**Empty it before the chapter ends.** Move the cash to BuckMe as soon as you can, and don’t leave money in the stash when you move on.

## After Chapter 1: BuckMe

You get the **BuckMe** app, your in-game bank, from the Chapter 1 mission **“Buck Me”**. After that:

1. Press **Up on the D-pad** to open your phone.
2. Open **BuckMe** and pick **ATM Finder**. It marks the nearest ATM.
3. Walk up to the ATM and choose **Deposit**.

**You can only deposit at an ATM.** The app finds ATMs but can’t move pocket cash into your account by itself.

**Check your balance** by pressing **Down on the D-pad**. It shows your pocket cash and your bank money.

## How BuckMe pays for things

- **Small purchases** (under about **$1,000**) use your pocket cash.
- **Anything bigger** comes straight out of BuckMe.
- **If you carry no cash**, purchases come from BuckMe automatically.

So there’s no reason to walk around with a full wallet.

## Family Savings

BuckMe also has a shared **Family Savings** account. Money you put in there **can’t be spent** later, so only put in what you mean to save. It’s never lost on death.

## Habits that save you money

- **Bank after every job.** Finish a mission, rob a store or sell a car, then go to an ATM.
- **Bank before you look for trouble.** Planning a robbery or a police chase? Empty your pockets first.
- **Sell loot quickly.** Stolen goods you’re carrying can be lost too.
- **Save before big missions.** GTA 6 has no mission replay, so a manual save protects both your progress and your cash.
