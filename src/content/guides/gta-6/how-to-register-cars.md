In GTA 6, driving off in a stolen car doesn’t make it yours. If you want to keep it, modify it and call it back later, you need to **register** it. When and where you can do that depends on how far you are in the story and on the car’s **tracker**.

## When can you register cars?

| Chapter | Where to register | What you can register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chapter 1** | Nowhere | Nothing yet |
| **Chapter 2** | **Moses** (Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn) | Cars with **no tracker** |
| **Early Chapter 3** | Any **Pay ’n’ Spray** | No tracker or **standard tracker** |
| **Late Chapter 3 or Chapter 4** | Any **Pay ’n’ Spray**, after **Randy’s Mamba GT** job | **Advanced tracker** cars too |

## What you need

- A car you can register at your stage of the story (see the table above). **Scan it with WAiNK** to see its tracker and the **registration cost**.
- **Money** for the registration fee. It depends on the car.
- **A free garage space** to keep it in. Each space lets you register one more car.

## How to register your first car (Chapter 2)

Registering unlocks with the **“Moses”** mission in Chapter 2, the same mission that gives you the [Slim Jim](/gta-6/how-to-get-slim-jim).

1. **Find a car with no tracker.** Scan cars with WAiNK (hold **Up on the D-pad**, then hold **R1 / RB**) until you find one that says **no tracker**.
2. **Drive it to Moses** at **Watkins Auto Parts** in **Port Gellhorn** (the **MW** icon on the map).
3. **Respray it first.** The **Register** option stays locked until you’ve given the car a new colour.
4. **Choose Register** instead of Sell.

That car is now yours.

## Registering at a Pay ’n’ Spray (Chapter 3)

From early **Chapter 3**, every **Pay ’n’ Spray** can register cars with **no tracker** or a **standard tracker**. Drive in, pay the fee and choose **Register**.

Registering a standard-tracker car also removes the restriction on modding it, so you can take it to a mod shop afterwards.

## Registering advanced-tracker cars (late in the story)

High-end cars with an **advanced tracker** can’t be registered until you’ve done enough work for **Randy**, who you meet in Chapter 3:

1. Work through his **Wish List** until he asks for a **’64 Declasse Mamba GT** from a mansion in **Leaf Links, Vice Beach**.
2. Deliver it, then **wait for Randy to call you** a few in-game hours later.
3. After that call, **any Pay ’n’ Spray** registers advanced-tracker cars.

Guides place this at the end of Chapter 3 or early in Chapter 4.

Until then you can drive these cars, but **keep your speed up**: an advanced tracker reports your location to the police whenever you drive too slowly. See [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

## Register or sell?

| | Register | Sell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You keep the car | **Yes** | No |
| You get money | No, **you pay** a fee | **Yes** |
| Best for | Cars you’ll actually drive | Everyday cars you stole for cash |

Not every buyer takes every car:

- **Moses** buys older, cheaper cars.
- **Pay ’n’ Sprays** buy standard-tracker cars, up to **three a day**.
- **Randy** only wants certain flashy, expensive cars.

WAiNK shows the sell value and which buyer suits the car. Full breakdown: [how to sell stolen cars in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-sell-cars).

## After you register

- **Store it in a garage.** Buy garages in the **Vice City Real Estate** app, which Moses tells you about after the Chapter 2 mission **“Daily Driver”**. Each garage you buy holds **one car**. A few free story garages hold two or three. In total you can keep about **18 cars**.
- **Call it to you.** Registered cars can be delivered to you through the **Scooter Bros** app.
- **Modify it.** Registered cars can go to mod shops, including ones that had a standard or advanced tracker.

> **Don’t quit before registering.** Cars you haven’t registered or stored can be lost when your session ends. Register (or at least park it safely) before you stop playing.

## Tips

- **Your first register is cheap practice.** Grab an untracked car you like on the way to the Moses mission and register it straight away.
- **Scan before you pay.** WAiNK shows the registration cost, so you won’t pay thousands for a car you’ll get bored of.
- **Use your free registration well.** Your **first car with Moses is registered for free**. Make it a good one, like [Paul Walker’s car](/gta-6/paul-walker-car), which you can steal before you meet him.
