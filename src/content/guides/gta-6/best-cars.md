GTA 6 doesn’t show a top speed in mph. Every car has **in-game ratings out of 10** for **Speed**, **Acceleration** and **Handling**, which you can see when you scan it with WAiNK or look at it in a mod shop. This guide ranks cars by those ratings, stock (no upgrades).

> **Heads up:** players are still finding new cars, so this list will change. These are the best confirmed so far.

## The catch: most top cars come from Randy

Almost every car at the top of this list is on **Randy’s Wish List**, a set of high-end cars Randy asks you to steal in **Chapter 3**. That means three things:

- You need the **[Key Cloner](/gta-6/how-to-get-key-cloner)**, because they’re **keyless**.
- Many have an **advanced tracker**: drive too slowly and the police get your location. See [trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).
- **Randy pays more for cars in perfect condition**, so repair any damage before you deliver one.

## Fastest overall: Truffade Thrax

The **Thrax** has the highest Speed rating found so far, **8.7**.

- **Where:** the car park of the **Dalton Island Club**. Take the **ferry from Waxman Island**.
- **When:** it only shows up **after you’ve collected every other car on Randy’s Wish List**. Randy then calls you about it.
- **Watch out:** there’s a guard to get past.

## Best all-rounder: Progen Emerus

The **Emerus** (Speed **8.3**, Handling **8.4**) is the best all-rounder on the list. You can get rare **“standout”** versions with their own paint and parts at fixed spots:

- **Royal Ruby Emerus** (blood red): parked at the **Vespucci Mansion** on **Vespucci Island**, close to Paul Walker’s car. It’s an illegal-operation site, so expect resistance.
- **Purple Drank Emerus** (purple, pearlescent): inside a **shipping container** at **Chatfield Storage & Logistics** on **Waxman Island**, also an illegal operation.

## Best acceleration: Pegassi Vitello

The **Vitello** has the best **acceleration** found so far (**8.4**) and Speed **8.2**.

- **Where:** a villa on **Flamingo Parkway** in **Waning Sands** (Leonard County, east of Belville). Facing the house, it’s in the **garage on the right, nearest the house**.
- **Needs:** the **Key Cloner**. It has an **advanced tracker**.
- It’s on **Randy’s Wish List**.

## Piccia Furia

Speed **7.8**, Acceleration **8.2**.

- **Where:** a mansion on **Palladia Lane** in **Peacock Estates** (Peacock Bay, Vice City). Skip the guarded gate: take the path on the **left and hop the hedge**. The car is in the garage.
- **Needs:** the **Key Cloner**.
- It’s on **Randy’s Wish List**.

## Best muscle car: Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire

The **Gauntlet Hellfire** is the fastest muscle car found so far, with Speed **7.8**, as fast as the Furia supercar.

- **Where:** the **M7 junkyard** in **Hamlet**, off Galerina Street, east of Boone Speedway. It’s a guarded spot, so be ready for a fight.
- It’s on **Randy’s Wish List**.

## Best free early car: Paul Walker’s Penumbra FF

Before Chapter 3 you won’t have the Key Cloner, so most supercars are off the table. The best car you can get early is the **Maibatsu ’97 Penumbra FF**, the green *Fast & Furious* tribute car:

- **Where:** a villa garage in the middle of **Vespucci Island**.
- **When:** as soon as you can roam freely.
- **Bonus:** bring it to Moses as your first car in Chapter 2 and he **registers it for free**.

Full guide: [how to get Paul Walker’s car in GTA 6](/gta-6/paul-walker-car).

## What are “standout” vehicles?

**Standout vehicles** are rare versions of normal cars, with their own paint, parts or liveries, and sometimes better stats. Most sit at **fixed spots** on the map: outside a house, in a garage, at a dock. The game tells you when you get into one. They’re worth grabbing, but check one before taking it to a mod shop, since its special parts can matter.

## Tips

- **Scan everything with WAiNK.** It shows a car’s ratings, tracker and value before you commit.
- **Plan around Chapter 3.** Most of the best cars need the Key Cloner, so don’t waste time chasing supercars in Chapters 1–2.
- **Drive carefully on delivery runs.** Supercars break easily, and Randy pays less for damaged cars.
- **Register the cars you love.** Registration for high-end cars can get expensive, so only keep the ones you’ll actually drive.
