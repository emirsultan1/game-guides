Stealing a car is only half the job. To turn it into money you need the right buyer, and in GTA 6 that depends on one thing: the car’s **tracker**.

## Who buys what

| Buyer | Where | Opens | Takes | Rough payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Moses** | Watkins Auto Parts, Port Gellhorn | **Chapter 2** | Cars with **no tracker** | **$1,000–$3,000** |
| **Pay ’n’ Spray** | Any Pay ’n’ Spray on the map | **Early Chapter 3** | **Standard-tracker** cars | **Around $4,000** for a good car |
| **Randy** | His garage in Vice City | **Mid Chapter 3** | Supercars on his **Wish List** | **$30,000+** |

Not sure which tracker a car has? Scan it with **WAiNK** before you take it: hold **Up on the D-pad**, aim at the car, then hold **R1 / RB**. More on that in [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

> **Daily limits:** Pay ’n’ Sprays only buy **three stolen cars per in-game day**, and Moses can run out of budget for the day too. If a buyer says no, come back the next day or try another buyer.

## Selling to Moses (Chapter 2)

Moses is your first buyer. He opens up with the **“Moses”** mission in Chapter 2, the same one that hands you the [Slim Jim](/gta-6/how-to-get-slim-jim).

1. Steal a car with **no tracker**. Older sedans and SUVs work best.
2. Drive it to **Watkins Auto Parts** in **Port Gellhorn** without crashing.
3. Talk to Moses and choose to get paid.

**What pays more:** a clean SUV usually beats a cheap hatchback for the same effort. You can check what he’ll pay by hovering over his shop on the map before you drive over.

**Moses won’t take tracked cars.** If it has a standard or advanced tracker, keep driving.

## Selling at a Pay ’n’ Spray (Chapter 3)

Once Chapter 3 opens it up, any Pay ’n’ Spray will buy cars with a **standard tracker**. These are the mid-range cars Moses turns away, and they’re worth a lot more.

1. Steal a **standard-tracker** car. It won’t call the police on you.
2. Drive into the nearest **Pay ’n’ Spray**.
3. Pick **Sell** from the menu and confirm. The money goes into your BuckMe account.

**Limit:** three stolen cars per in-game day. After that, wait for the next day.

There are Pay ’n’ Sprays all over the map, so this is a quick loop in Chapter 3: steal, short drive, sell. Do your three a day, then switch to something else.

**Later on:** after Randy’s **Mamba GT** job, Pay ’n’ Sprays also buy cars with **advanced trackers**.

## Selling to Randy (mid Chapter 3)

Randy is the big money. He only wants specific high-end cars from his **Wish List**, and he pays **$30,000 or more** for each.

The catch: these cars come with **advanced trackers**, so the police find you if you drive too slowly. Full details in the [Randy’s Wish List guide](/gta-6/randys-wish-list).

## How to get the best price

- **Don’t crash.** Every dent lowers the payout. Take the long, empty road over the short, busy one.
- **Scan first.** WAiNK tells you the tracker and the value before you commit.
- **Pick the right buyer.** Taking a standard-tracker car to Moses wastes the trip.
- **Bank the cash.** Money in your pocket is lost if you die or get arrested. See [how to keep your money safe](/gta-6/keep-money-safe).

## Sell or keep?

If you like the car, [register it](/gta-6/how-to-register-cars) instead. Registering costs money but the car is yours to keep and modify. Selling gives you cash but the car is gone.
