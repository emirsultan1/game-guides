The **Slim Jim** is the first car-theft upgrade in GTA 6. Before you have it, the only way into a locked car is to smash the window, which sets off the alarm on almost every car. With it, you can take cars quietly.

## Where to get the Slim Jim

You can’t buy it or find it lying around. It’s a **story unlock**:

1. Play into **Chapter 2**.
2. Finish the mission **“Girls’ Trip”**.
3. The next mission, **“Moses”**, unlocks. It’s the **fifth story mission of Chapter 2**.
4. Go to Moses’ garage, **Watkins Auto Parts** in **Port Gellhorn**. Look for the **MW** icon on the map.
5. Moses gives Lucia the Slim Jim and walks you through how to use it.

**There’s no way to get it early.** If you’re still in Chapter 1, keep playing the story.

Once it’s unlocked, **both Jason and Lucia** can use it.

## How to use the Slim Jim

1. Walk up to the driver’s door of a locked car.
2. Press **Triangle / Y** to use the Slim Jim instead of breaking the window.
3. Complete the short **stick minigame** to pop the lock.
4. Get in and **hotwire** the car as normal.

It’s slower than smashing the glass. In return, **the alarm doesn’t go off**, so nobody calls the police about it.

### Opening trunks

You can also use the Slim Jim on a car’s **trunk** to look inside without setting off the alarm, even if you’re not planning to take the car.

## What the Slim Jim can’t do

- **Keyless cars:** it can open the door, but these cars have no ignition to hotwire, so you can’t drive them away. You need the **[Key Cloner](/gta-6/how-to-get-key-cloner)** from Chapter 3. If WAiNK says **Keyless Entry**, the Slim Jim isn’t the right tool.
- **Cars with someone inside:** you can’t Slim Jim an occupied car. Carjack it or move on.
- **Trackers:** the Slim Jim gets you in quietly, but it does nothing about a car’s tracker. See [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

## Slim Jim vs. smashing the window

| | Smash the window | Slim Jim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available | From the start | Chapter 2 |
| Speed | Fast | Slower (minigame) |
| Sets off the alarm | Yes | **No** |
| Works on keyless cars | Gets you in, but you can’t drive away | Same: you can’t drive away |
| Best for | Cars with no alarm, or when nobody’s around | Alarmed cars in busy areas |

## Tips

- **Scan with WAiNK first** (hold **Up on the D-pad**, then hold **R1 / RB**). It tells you if the car is alarmed or keyless before you waste time.
- **Use it in busy areas.** An alarm in a crowded street means witnesses and a police call. The Slim Jim avoids that.
- **Pair it with Moses.** In Chapter 2, Moses is also where you **register** your first stolen cars. See [how to register cars in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-register-cars).
