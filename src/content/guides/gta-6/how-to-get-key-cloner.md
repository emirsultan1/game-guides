Newer and more expensive cars in GTA 6 are **keyless**. There’s no ignition to hotwire, so even if you smash the window and climb in, **you can’t drive away**. The **Key Cloner** fixes that. It’s the tool you need for most sports cars, supercars and electric cars.

## When you get the Key Cloner

It’s a **story unlock**. You can’t buy it or find it early.

- **Chapter:** around the middle of **Chapter 3**, well into the story.
- **Mission:** Randy’s job **“Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs”**. Some guides and the in-game objective call it **“Steal the Zorrusso for Randy”**. It’s the same mission.
- **What happens:** Randy calls near the start of the mission and has you add the Key Cloner to your phone. Your first use is stealing a **Zorrusso** in **Ocean Beach** for him.

After that mission, the Key Cloner is yours **permanently**.

## How to use the Key Cloner

1. **Scan the car with WAiNK** (hold **Up on the D-pad**, then hold **R1 / RB**). If it says **keyless**, you need the Key Cloner.
2. Walk up to the **driver’s door**.
3. Choose **Clone Key** (**Triangle / Y**).
4. The door unlocks and the car **starts automatically**. Drive off.

## Watch the tracker

Keyless cars are usually the expensive ones, and expensive cars often have an **advanced tracker**. Even the very first Zorrusso you steal for Randy has one.

With an advanced tracker, **don’t slow down**. If you drive too slowly, it beeps and gives you a few seconds to speed up. If you don’t, it **sends your location to the police**. Plan a route with open roads before you take the car.

More on this: [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

## Key Cloner vs. Slim Jim

| | Slim Jim | Key Cloner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlocks | Chapter 2 (Moses) | Chapter 3 (Randy) |
| Works on | Locked, alarmed cars with a normal ignition | **Keyless** cars |
| Starts the car | No, you still hotwire | **Yes**, automatically |
| Sets off the alarm | No | No |

You’ll use both. The Slim Jim for everyday cars, the Key Cloner for the high-end ones.
