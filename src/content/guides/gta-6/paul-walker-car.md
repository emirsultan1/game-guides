GTA 6 has a tribute to **Paul Walker’s** bright green car from the first *Fast & Furious* movie. In the game it’s called the **Maibatsu ’97 Penumbra FF**: same green paint, blue graphics and big rear wing. It’s free, you can grab it very early, and it’s one of the best cars you can have at the start of the game.

## Where is Paul Walker’s car?

- **Island:** Vespucci Island, one of the **Rialto Islands**, between mainland Vice City and Vice Beach.
- **Spot:** a **villa garage** near the **middle of the island**, just off the main road that crosses it.
- **How to recognise the villa:** look for a **small side door right next to the garage door**.

If the island is still fogged on your map, just drive around the area and it clears.

## When can you get it?

**Right away.** No mission, purchase or trophy unlocks it. It’s parked there from the moment you can roam the map freely.

You *can* take it straight away, but there’s a good reason to time it (see [register it for free](#register-it-for-free) below).

## How to get it out of the garage

1. **Get onto the property.** Hop over the gate.
2. **Break open the side door** next to the garage. This **sets off the house alarm**.
3. **Turn the alarm off fast.** The alarm panel is on the wall inside the garage. Switch it off right away so the police don’t get involved.
4. **Open the garage door** with the switch on the wall.
5. **Get into the car.** It’s locked:
   - **From the start of the game:** smash the window and hotwire it (line up the two markers with **both sticks**).
   - **From Chapter 2:** use the **[Slim Jim](/gta-6/how-to-get-slim-jim)** instead. It opens the door without breaking glass or setting off the car alarm.
6. **Drive off.**

> **Be quick.** In GTA 6 the police only get involved when there’s a witness or an alarm. Breaking the side door sets one off, so the faster you kill it, the cleaner your getaway.

## Register it for free

Driving off doesn’t make the car yours. If you don’t register it, you can **lose it** when your session ends. Registering it normally costs about **$6,750**, but there’s a free way:

1. **Steal the Penumbra FF before your first visit to Moses**, in Chapter 2.
2. Bring it to Moses at **Watkins Auto Parts** in **Port Gellhorn** during the **“Moses”** mission.
3. **Your first car with Moses is registered for free.** Use that one-time offer on this car.

**Already used your free registration?** You can still keep it. You just pay the normal fee, at Moses or later at a **Pay ’n’ Spray**. See [how to register cars in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-register-cars).

## Is it worth it?

Yes, especially early on:

- It **handles really well** and is **much faster than the starter cars**.
- It’s **free**, and with Moses’ offer, so is registering it.
- It’s a great-looking car to mod once it’s registered.

Later in the game you’ll find faster supercars (see [the best cars in GTA 6](/gta-6/best-cars)), but nothing beats the Penumbra FF for the first few chapters.

## Tips

- **Grab it on your way to Moses.** If you’re about to start Chapter 2, make the detour first so the free registration goes on this car and not something boring.
- **Don’t quit before registering.** Park it safely and register it as soon as you can.
- **Wait for the Slim Jim if you like.** Stealing it in Chapter 2 with the Slim Jim means no car alarm, and you’ll go straight to Moses anyway.
