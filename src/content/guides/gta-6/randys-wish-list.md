Randy’s Wish List is the best car money in GTA 6 before bank vaults. He wants specific supercars, and he pays **$30,000 or more** for each one. The hard part is finding them and getting them to him in one piece.

## How the Wish List works

- **When it opens:** in **Chapter 3**, through Randy’s story missions. Your first delivery happens as part of a mission.
- **How many cars:** about **20**. A few get added later, like the **’64 Declasse Mamba GT**.
- **How to track it:** open your phone’s **Touch Pad → Robberies → Randy’s Wish List** to see which cars you still need.
- **Payout:** **$30,000+ per car**, less if it’s damaged.

Some of these come through Randy’s missions with a marked location. The rest you hunt down yourself.

The **Truffade Thrax** is the last one. Randy only tells you where it is, the **Dalton Island Club** (by ferry from Waxman Island), after you’ve delivered every other car.

## How to find the cars

Most Wish List cars spawn at **fixed spots**, but they show as **location unknown** at first. **Randy texts you a hint** about where to look, so read his messages.

A few general rules help:

- **Rich neighbourhoods first.** Mansions and gated houses, especially around **Capri** and **Vice Beach**, are where supercars park.
- **Time of day matters.** Some cars only show up at certain hours. The **Pegassi Toros**, for example, parks outside **Pegassi Residences** on Bayside Dr in **Tequesta Retreat** around **6:30 PM**.
- **Use Focus Mode** (press both sticks) when you think you’re close. It highlights useful things nearby.
- **Scan before you steal.** WAiNK confirms it’s the right car and shows what you need to get in.

## How to deliver without losing money

Every Wish List car is a high-end car, which means two problems: it’s probably **keyless**, and it has an **advanced tracker**.

**Getting in:** keyless cars need the **Key Cloner**. See [how to get the Key Cloner](/gta-6/how-to-get-key-cloner).

**Getting it home:**

1. **Plan your route first.** Highways and long straight roads. Avoid downtown traffic.
2. **Keep your speed up.** Slow down and the tracker starts beeping. You get a few seconds to speed up before the police get your location.
3. **Don’t crash.** Damage cuts the payout, and a crash usually means slowing down too.
4. **Drive straight to Randy.** No detours.

More on escaping: [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

## Tips

- **Do Wish List cars when you’re fresh.** These drives are tense. Don’t start one at the end of a session.
- **Bank your $30,000 straight away.** Pocket cash is lost if you die or get arrested. See [how to keep your money safe](/gta-6/keep-money-safe).
- **Want to keep one?** Cars you deliver are gone. But after Randy’s Mamba GT job and his follow-up call, any Pay ’n’ Spray can [register](/gta-6/how-to-register-cars) advanced-tracker cars, so you can steal a second copy for yourself.
