Stealing cars in GTA 6 isn’t just “press Y and drive off” anymore. Most parked cars are **locked**, nearly all have an **alarm**, newer ones are **keyless**, and expensive ones carry **trackers** that can call the police on you.

The good news: once you know which tool each car needs, it’s easy. This guide covers every method, in the order the game unlocks them.

## Step 1: Scan the car with WAiNK first

Before you touch a car, scan it. **WAiNK** is an app on your phone that tells you everything about a car before you commit:

- Make and model
- Whether it’s **locked**, **alarmed** or **keyless**
- Whether it has **no tracker**, a **standard tracker** or an **advanced tracker**
- Its condition, what it **sells for**, and what it **costs to register**

**How to scan:** hold **Up on the D-pad** to bring up WAiNK, aim your phone at the car, then hold **R1 / RB**.

You get WAiNK early. Cal helps you install it during **“Unwanted Attention”**, the sixth story mission of Chapter 1.

> **Tip:** scanning takes two seconds and tells you which tool you need, whether you’ll have the police on you, and whether the car is worth keeping. Make it a habit.

## Step 2: Pick your way in

You can also just walk up to the driver’s door and press the interact button. The prompt shows if the car is locked, alarmed or keyless.

### Smash the window and hotwire (from the start)

This works from the very beginning of the game on any car with a normal ignition.

1. Smash the window to get in.
2. Hotwire it: use **both sticks** to find the sweet spot. It only takes a second or two.

The downside: on an alarmed car, smashing the window **sets off the alarm**. Witnesses notice, people call the police, and you get attention you didn’t need.

### Slim Jim (Chapter 2)

The **Slim Jim** opens a locked door **without breaking the glass or setting off the alarm**. It’s slower than smashing, but quiet. You also use it to open a car’s **trunk** without triggering the alarm.

- **When you get it:** Chapter 2, from Moses, the mechanic in Port Gellhorn, during the mission **“Moses”**.
- **How to use it:** at a locked car door, press **Triangle / Y** and complete the short stick minigame. Then hotwire the car as normal.
- **What it can’t do:** it won’t start **keyless** cars, and it doesn’t work on a car with someone sitting in it.

Full details: [How to get the Slim Jim in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-get-slim-jim).

### Key Cloner (Chapter 3)

High-end and newer cars are **keyless**. They have no ignition to hotwire, so smashing the window gets you inside but **you can’t drive away**. For these you need the **Key Cloner**.

- **When you get it:** Chapter 3, during Randy’s mission **“Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs”** (it also shows as **“Steal the Zorrusso for Randy”**).
- **How to use it:** stand by the driver’s door of a keyless car and choose **Clone Key** (**Triangle / Y**). It unlocks the door and starts the car for you.

Full details: [How to get the Key Cloner in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-get-key-cloner).

### Carjacking

You can still pull drivers out of their cars, but it’s riskier than in older GTA games:

- Many drivers **fight back**. Mash the prompt button quickly to win the struggle. Lose it and they knock you down and drive off.
- Some drivers are **armed** and will shoot.
- Carjacking happens in front of witnesses, so expect the police to hear about it.

## Which tool does each car need?

| What the scan shows | How to take it | Available from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlocked | Just get in (hotwire if needed) | Start of the game |
| Locked, no alarm | Smash the window + hotwire | Start of the game |
| Locked + alarmed | **Slim Jim** to stay quiet, or smash and accept the alarm | Slim Jim: Chapter 2 |
| Keyless | **Key Cloner** only | Chapter 3 |

## Step 3: Check the tracker before you drive off

The tracker decides whether you can keep the car and how hot your escape will be:

- **No tracker:** easy to keep or sell.
- **Standard tracker:** you can’t take it to a mod shop until it’s registered, but it won’t bring the police.
- **Advanced tracker:** found on high-end cars. If you drive **too slowly**, it starts beeping and gives you a few seconds to speed up. If you don’t, it **gives your location to the police**.

Full details: [GTA 6 trackers explained](/gta-6/car-trackers-explained).

## Step 4: Keep it or sell it

A stolen car isn’t yours just because you drove it away. To keep it, you need to **register** it, and to store it you need a **free garage space**.

- **Register it** to make it your personal vehicle. You can start registering cars in **Chapter 2** with Moses, at **Pay ’n’ Sprays** from **Chapter 3**, and advanced-tracker cars only after a later job for Randy. See [how to register cars in GTA 6](/gta-6/how-to-register-cars).
- **Sell it** if you just want the money. Different buyers want different cars:
  - **Moses** buys older, cheaper cars.
  - **Pay ’n’ Sprays** buy most stolen cars.
  - **Randy** only wants certain flashy, expensive cars.

WAiNK tells you which buyer a car suits.

## Tips

- **Scan first, always.** Smashing into a keyless car before you have the Key Cloner just wastes time and draws attention.
- **Quiet beats fast.** Once you have the Slim Jim, use it on alarmed cars in busy areas. No alarm means fewer witnesses calling it in.
- **With an advanced tracker, keep moving.** Plan a route without traffic lights and tight corners before you take the car.
- **Don’t wreck your prize.** Driving fast to beat an advanced tracker can damage the car before you get it to a garage.
